# Retrospective: the GNOME toast and popup are sized and drawn in cells

Last verified at commit `7e8cb508508c96c9fcf03e57231e427b8a8790b5`.

The task fixed a GNOME message toast that clipped the last character of a line. Plan 1 found two causes: `cmdline_ui::toast_width_cols` counted characters rather than grid cells, and `draw_toast` drew each chunk as one Pango run, whose whole-pixel-rounded advance outgrows a box sized in the unrounded `cell_w`. The completion popup shared both faults and got the same fix. The work is `75fae67` (code and tests) and `7e8cb50` (documentation).

The run went straight through: `architect-1`, `test-writer-1`, `coder-1`, `technical-writer-1` and `reviewer-1`, with one plan and one review. The five invocations cost $13.93: `architect-1` $5.70 over 61 turns and 18 minutes, `test-writer-1` $2.34, `reviewer-1` $2.23, `coder-1` $1.87 and `technical-writer-1` $1.80. Both gates passed first time on both workers, so nothing below cost an extra invocation.

## What went well

- **`cmdline_ui` is a pure module, so the width rule took plain unit tests.** C1a to C5 sit in its existing `mod tests`, and `coder-1` showed C4 and C5 red against the unchanged width functions with one `cargo test -p wolf-gnome --lib cmdline_ui`. Its module doc's "No cairo, no pango, no widget" held, because GLib's `Unichar` tables need no display.
- **`render.rs` already recorded the rounding hazard.** The `GlyphPainter` doc said a run advances by "the face's own hinted advance" rather than `metrics.cell_w`, which is why `paint` draws one cell at a time. Plan 1 §1 cited it as the second cause, and `draw_cells` reuses the same painter.
- **`paint_golden.rs` gave the offscreen paint test its pattern.** Plan 1 pointed `tests/cell_text_paint.rs` at its `probe_metrics` idiom and the pinned fontconfig. The test writer built C6 and its precondition on an `ImageSurface` with no window, Neovim or compositor, and C6 went red under the one-run edit in `coder-1`'s falsifier run.
- **The existing message smoke took the end-to-end width check.** The test writer added a third, wide line to `MSG_SCENARIO` in `smoke_headless/editor.rs` and read the toast's box from the capture's pixels, reusing `decode_capture`, `cell_metrics_for_smoke` and `OVERLAY_ORIGIN_X_EXPECTED`. It showed 237 px against 372 on the base code.
- **`build.md` settled the wiring in one line.** The instructions said to wire each new test file into its Bazel target. `build.md` says `wolf-gnome` is deliberately given no Bazel target, so plan 1 and review 1 §3 settled that `cell_text_paint.rs` needs no wiring without a search.
- **Planned word counts let the writer land in one pass.** Plan 1 §5 gave each entry's `--entry-words` count (`cmdline_ui.rs` 206, `render.rs` 248). `technical-writer-1` landed at 244 and 249 of 250 in 45 turns ($1.80), where the writer of [application-state-10](application-state-10.md) took 68 against entries at their limit.

## What did not go well

- **The architect could not measure the premise the C6 test rests on.** Plan 1 §4 said DejaVu Sans Mono's 12 pt advance "is expected to round to 10". `architect-1`'s system prompt has no `regina on linux`, and the macOS worker has neither `cargo` nor the `glib` crate's source, so it read the `Unichar` trait on docs.rs through two `WebFetch` calls and searched the web for Pango's glyph rounding; the premise held. This is recommendation 1 of [round-3-2](round-3-2.md), come up again.
- **The paths that matter most to the bug have no test.** The smokes run at `SMOKE_FONT_PT` 28, where DejaVu's 22.48 px advance rounds down, so a run never clips there. Nothing observes that `draw_toast` and `draw_pum` call `draw_cells`, or that `draw_pum` calls `clip_to_cells`; review 1 §3 checked both by reading them. They are private methods of the 6,040-line `pane_view.rs`, so `cell_text_paint.rs` cannot reach them.
- **The base named by the mechanics was ten tasks back.** `e0b759ae` is 41 commits below `HEAD`. `technical-writer-1` and review 1 each re-derived that this task is `75fae67` and `7e8cb50`. This is recommendation 3 of [round-3-10](round-3-10.md) and 6 of [application-state-10](application-state-10.md), come up again.
- **The wolf-gnome READMEs grew further past the size warning.** `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md` went from 128,401 to 129,966 bytes and `crates/wolf-gnome/src/README.md` from 97,147 to 97,416, against the 50,000-byte `big-file` warning. `technical-writer-1` cut the `render.rs` entry's other bullets to fit `draw_cells`. This is recommendation 5 of [application-state-9](application-state-9.md) and of [application-state-10](application-state-10.md), come up again.
- **Duplicated sizing code tripped a falsifier.** `place_toast` and `place_pum` in `crates/wolf-gnome/src/pane_view.rs` share the line `let w = (cols as f64).mul_add(...)` word for word. `coder-1`'s scripted C8b edit refused the non-unique match on its first run and had to be widened.
- **Review 1 left three small defects.** The C8a failure message in `smoke_headless/editor.rs` says the buggy box "is sized for 8 cells", where it is sized for the 10-cell `victorlima` line. The `GlyphDecoration` doc in `crates/wolf-gnome/src/glyph_style.rs` still says `draw_glyph` builds no `AttrList`, when `GlyphPainter::draw` does; `technical-writer-1` fixed the same claim in `tests/README.md` but could not touch source. The `cmdline_columns` doc says "the same approximation the grid paint makes", which the grid paint does not make.

## Recommendations

1. **Give the toast's and popup's drawing a seam the offscreen test can call.** Move the bodies of `draw_toast` and `draw_pum` in `crates/wolf-gnome/src/pane_view.rs` into `pub` functions of `render.rs` or a small module, taking a `cairo::Context`, `CellMetrics` and the lines. `tests/cell_text_paint.rs` could then draw a whole toast at 12 pt and see its last cell inside the box, which covers the call sites review 1 §3 could only read.
2. **Give the architect `regina on linux`.** This is recommendation 1 of [round-3-2](round-3-2.md) and [followups-3](followups-3.md), come up again. Here it would have measured the 12 pt rounding and read `glib::Unichar` from the crate source instead of docs.rs.
3. **Record the task's start commit once.** This is recommendation 3 of [round-3-10](round-3-10.md), come up again in `technical-writer-1` and review 1.
4. **Split the wolf-gnome READMEs.** This is recommendation 5 of [application-state-9](application-state-9.md), come up again with `tests/README.md` at 129,966 bytes.
5. **Mend the three small defects review 1 found.** Make the C8a message in `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/smoke_headless/editor.rs` say the box is sized for the widest line by character count, name `GlyphPainter::draw` in the `GlyphDecoration` doc in `crates/wolf-gnome/src/glyph_style.rs`, and drop "the same approximation the grid paint makes" from `cmdline_columns`'s doc in `crates/wolf-gnome/src/cmdline_ui.rs`.
