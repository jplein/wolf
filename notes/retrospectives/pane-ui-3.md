# Task 3 of pane-ui: add the Split Horizontal and Split Vertical palette commands

Last verified at commit `484222a14e2659aca58530807fef9b668caf254a`.

The task added `Command::SplitHorizontal` (28) and `Command::SplitVertical` (29) with `:WolfSplitHorizontal` and `:WolfSplitVertical`, `WolfCore::split_pane`, the Lua helper `wolf_split_pane` and the `wolf_terminal_split` notification, and the FFI, macOS and GNOME dispatch. It added `crates/wolf-core/tests/split_commands.rs` (S1 to S7), `SplitCommandTests.swift` (M1 to M8) and `smoke_headless/split_commands.rs` (G1 to G3). The commits are `ac34e5f` (code and tests) and `484222a` (documentation).

Five invocations cost about $27.40 and 61 minutes: `architect-1` $10.26, `test-writer-1` $5.39, `coder-1` $4.62, `technical-writer-1` $3.46 and `reviewer-1` $3.67. There was no round trip. Both gates passed on both workers on their first run, and review 1 approved with minor notes.

## What went well

- **The chords' split path took the commands as they are.** `wolf_do_split` in `runtime/nvim/init.lua` and `router_split` in `crates/wolf-core/src/terminal.rs` already gave the right pane for an editor, a preview and a terminal, and `router_split` already refused the drawer. So the Lua helper is an eight-line function, and plan 1 §1 could predict from the code that a preview needs no code of its own. S5, S6, M5, M6 and G3 passed with none.
- **The ordered input channel settled the focus lag without new design.** `InputEvent::SplitPane` queues behind any `FocusWin`, and `PaneContainerView.splitActivePane(_:)` copies `closeActivePane()`'s focus-first shape. Plan 1 cited both precedents, and M8 under E9 (C20) shows the order matters.
- **The checklist in `specs/command-lifecycle.md` §8 named every site to touch.** Plan 1 §3 follows its shared steps one by one, and no gate run, review or falsifier found a site missed. The append-only guards it lists (the `0..=N` array in `crates/wolf-ffi/tests/commands.rs`, `CommandSeamTests`, the last-row guard) made E12 and E13 cheap to falsify.
- **The test harnesses were reused, not rebuilt.** S1 to S7 use `crates/wolf-core/tests/common/mod.rs` (`start_core_with_temp_md`, `open_session`, `poll_until`), M1 to M8 use `TerminalViewFixture` and `PreviewViewFixture`, and G1 to G3 reuse `pin_terminal_smoke_shell`, `leaf_fields` and `PREVIEW_QUERY`. The GNOME smoke is one file and one `mod` line, which [pane-ui-2](pane-ui-2.md) also credited to [ghostty-4](ghostty-4.md) recommendation 2.

## What did not go well

- **The command registry is restated in about eleven places, and most of the diff there only bumps a number.** Appending two commands, `test-writer-1` and `coder-1` between them changed these sites, each by hand:
  - in `crates/wolf-core/src/command.rs`, `EXPECTED_COMMAND_COUNT` and the `Command` doc's discriminant list;
  - `STATIC_COMMAND_COUNT` and its doc in `crates/wolf-core/tests/palette_rank.rs`;
  - in `crates/wolf-ffi/tests/commands.rs`, the local `CMD_KIND_*` constants, the module doc and the `0..=29` array, and in `crates/wolf-ffi/src/lib.rs` the two `NEW_WINDOW .. SPLIT_VERTICAL` comments;
  - the header and `init?(ffi:)` docs in `CommandSeam.swift`, and the out-of-range probe, 28 to 30, in `CommandSeamTests.swift`;
  - `EXPECTED_DISPATCHED_IDS: [&str; 26]` and its "twenty-six" message in `crates/wolf-gnome/src/command_action.rs`, and a "27" in `notes/doc-refactor-misc-recommendations.md`.
- **Each new command costs three boilerplate tests and a rename.** `command.rs` gained `split_*_id_round_trips`, `split_*_spec_fields` and `split_*_is_at_index_*` for each command, and `edit_in_other_pane_is_the_last_row` became `edit_in_other_pane_is_at_index_27`, all because the §8 shared steps ask for them. Review 1 §4 found two of the bumped tables (the `CASES` rows of `ui_action_table`, the `CommandSeamTests` probe) without a falsifier ID, and had to argue that they were not materially changed.
- **The architect was the costliest invocation, by a wide margin.** `architect-1` took $10.26, 118 turns and 19 minutes, 37% of the task, in 112 shell calls over more than 30 source and test files across core, `init.lua`, the bridge, AppKit and GTK. Much of that was finding each mirror above, and checking the two new titles against ten fuzzy queries other tests pin (plan 1 §3 lists `prv`, `prvan`, `ednap`, `fullw`, `orzulu`, `zephyrdock`, `pvovsrc` and others).
- **Pinned palette queries constrain every new title, and the guard against a collision is split.** `PREVIEW_SMOKE_QUERIES` in `crates/wolf-gnome/src/palette.rs` pins `prv` at exactly 3 actionable rows, so any new title with `p…r…v` in it breaks a preview test. Plan 1 kept `:vsplit` out of the Split Vertical label for that reason. The new `splitvert` and `splithor` queries got their own uniqueness check inside the cage smoke (`assert_split_queries_are_unique`) rather than a row in that fast table.
- **Three spec sentences went stale, and no agent could fix them.** `specs/architecture.md` ("only the rebound chords and Ex-commands emit") and `specs/terminal-panes.md` ("Splitting a terminal pane, with `<C-w>s` or `<C-w>v`") no longer name every trigger. `specs/command-lifecycle.md` §8(b) step 1 says the handle calls `send_input`, while §7 and these commands go through a `WolfCore` wrapper. Plan 1 §1, `technical-writer-1` and review 1 §8.3 each recorded them.
- **The §8(b) heading lists its members, so its anchor moves with each new member.** The task's one spec edit turned `(b) Editor-affecting (NewBuffer, Terminal)` into a four-name heading, and `coder-1` had to grep for links to the old anchor (none existed).
- **Three README entries were at the 250-word budget again.** The `api.rs` and `terminal.rs` entries of `crates/wolf-core/src/README.md` and the `PaneContainerView.swift` entry of `crates/wolf-macos/Sources/WolfMacOS/README.md` were at 248 to 250, and `technical-writer-1` reworded neighbouring bullets to fit one clause each. Review 1 §5 noted that the `api.rs` bullet lost "detects stalls". `crates/wolf-core/tests/README.md` is 59,882 bytes, over the size warning, and the `WolfMacOS` README 154 bytes under it.
- **Small defects landed, as review 1 §8 found.** The `TERMINAL_SPLIT_EVENT` arm in `crates/wolf-core/src/redraw.rs` says "No snapshot lock", though `router_split` takes a read lock through `placeholder_win`. The drawer barriers in both new test files say the landed focus means Neovim "handled" both splits, which holds for the Lua side only, and the core one reads "so it landing". The `notes/feat-list.md` row cites `ac34e5f`, which holds only if the branch keeps its commits.
- **A leaf walker was written a second time.** `split_leaves` in `smoke_headless/split_commands.rs` is `collect_leaves` in `smoke_headless/terminal.rs` with a different output type. Both wrap `leaf_fields` from `main.rs`.
- **`regina on linux` output order cost reading time again.** `coder-1` reported that stdout comes before stderr, so `| tail` on a cargo run shows only compile lines. This is [open-items-13](open-items-13.md) recommendation 3, come up again.

## Recommendations

1. **Derive the registry mirrors from `COMMAND_SPECS`.** This takes [round-3-11](round-3-11.md) recommendation 7, on prose counts, to test constants. Keep `EXPECTED_COMMAND_COUNT` in `command.rs` as the one hand-kept count, set `STATIC_COMMAND_COUNT` in `palette_rank.rs` to `COMMAND_SPECS.len() - 1`, and build the `0..=N` array in `crates/wolf-ffi/tests/commands.rs` from `COMMAND_SPECS`. Drop the discriminant lists and counts from the doc comments of `Command`, `CommandSeam.swift` and `commands.rs`, and from the two `lib.rs` comments; this task edited about eleven such sites by hand.
2. **Replace the per-command tests in `command.rs` with one table test.** One test that walks `COMMAND_SPECS` and checks each row's index equals its discriminant and its id round-trips through `Command::from_id` covers what `split_*_id_round_trips` and `split_*_is_at_index_*` cover, for every command at once. Keep one last-row guard that reads the last variant, not a named one. Update §8's shared steps in `specs/command-lifecycle.md` to match; that needs a task that opens the spec.
3. **Collect every pinned palette query into the one fast table.** Move the smokes' query constants (`PREVIEW_QUERY`, `EDIT_QUERY`, `splitvert`, `splithor` and the rest) into `PREVIEW_SMOKE_QUERIES` in `crates/wolf-gnome/src/palette.rs`, or a sibling table, and assert there that each selects its command. A new title that collides would then fail in `cargo test --lib`, and the architect would not need to check ten queries by hand, as plan 1 §3 did.
4. **Open the three stale spec sentences in one task.** Name `specs/architecture.md` "Split panes — present limitations", the trigger sentence of `specs/terminal-panes.md`, and step 1 of §8(b) in `specs/command-lifecycle.md`, with the wording `technical-writer-1` proposed for the last ("calls the `WolfCore` wrapper of step 2, or `send_input` when there is none"). In the same edit, drop the member lists from the three §8 flavor headings so their anchors stop moving.
5. **Mend review 1's three leftovers.** Reword the `TERMINAL_SPLIT_EVENT` comment in `redraw.rs` to "No snapshot write, no `seq` bump, no wake", fix "so it landing" and narrow "Neovim handled them" in `split_commands.rs` and `SplitCommandTests.swift`, and make the `notes/feat-list.md` row cite no commit. Fold `split_leaves` into a shared walker in `smoke_headless/main.rs` beside `leaf_fields`.
6. **Make room in the entries this request keeps editing.** This is [pane-ui-1](pane-ui-1.md) recommendation 1 and [pane-ui-2](pane-ui-2.md) recommendation 3, come up again, now with the `api.rs` and `terminal.rs` entries of `crates/wolf-core/src/README.md` at the budget too. The zoom tasks of this request will touch the same three entries.
