# Retrospective: keep the tab's last pane beside the terminal drawer

Last verified at commit `3eacfa660aced1174eeb01610710ecb229e8d341`.

The task stopped `:bd`, `:bw`, the quit family and Close Pane from closing the tab's only pane beside the terminal drawer, and sent files from the picker, the sidebar and the Open dialog to an editor pane when the drawer is active. It took two rounds and ten invocations, about $39.36 and 107 minutes of agent time.

Round 1 (`architect-1` to `reviewer-1`) cost $31.85. Review 1 sent it back for two defects in the new `:bd` helper, and round 2 (`architect-2` to `reviewer-2`) cost $7.51. No gate failed, and there was no discrepancy or resume.

## What went well

- **Every close path already went through `runtime/nvim/init.lua`, so "last pane" became one rule.** `do_quit`, `do_close` and `wolf_terminal_close` each counted `nvim_tabpage_list_wins(0)`, so plan 1 §3 replaced the three counts with one `closable(win)`. Core's `InputEvent::ClosePane` could join them by calling `wolf_do_close()` over `nvim_exec_lua`, as `new_untitled` already does. Review 1 §1 found this part right on the first pass.
- **Both front-ends already had one placement route for `wolf FILE`.** `PaneContainerView.openFile` and `PaneInner::pending_file_opens` place a file by `specs/cli-and-file-open.md` §5.2, which never picks the drawer. So plan 1 sent picked files through it with one shared entry per front-end, `openPickedFile` and `TabsHandle::drawer_opener`, and review 1 accepted both.
- **The wired real-Neovim targets kept the tests and falsifiers cheap.** `drawer_last_pane` copied `terminal_drawer`'s `rust_test`, and every core falsifier was a one-line edit of `init.lua`. `coder-1` reported a red for each of 31 IDs. In round 2 the target was already wired, and C27 reused the `delete_beside_the_drawer` helper, so `test-writer-2` cost $1.16 and `coder-2` $1.05.

## What did not go well

- **The one round trip came from porting Neovim's buffer pick into Lua.** Review 1 found two defects in `do_bdelete` and `bdelete_replacement`, and the five round-2 invocations cost $7.51 and 23 minutes.
  - §2.1: after the helper switched the window, `bdelete %` ran from the raw `o.args`, and `%` by then named the replacement, which brought the task's bug back. Plan 1 §3 step 5.1 prescribed `o.args`. `coder-1` fixed the bare `:bd` case, which has the same cause, but not `%`. Nothing in the repository invited this, and nothing is recommended for it.
  - §2.2: the port of `mark_jumplist_forget_file` decremented the jumplist index wrongly when two deleted entries preceded it.
- **No test could tell the port from a simpler rule.** Review 2 §3 found that in C1 to C4 and C28 Neovim's pick is also the first loaded buffer after the deleted one by number, which is `bdelete_replacement`'s fallback. So deleting the whole jumplist walk in `init.lua` still passes every test. Only C28, which runs the same keys in a drawer-closed core and beside the drawer and compares the result, could catch §2.2, and it was written only after review 1 asked for it.
- **Three documents named the routes into one queue, and plan 1 listed none of them.** `PaneContainerView.pendingFileOpens`, `openFile` and `PaneInner::pending_file_opens` said every queued file comes "from outside Wolf". `notes/wolf-gnome/crash-and-stall-recovery.md` said only the `wolf` command reaches `WolfCore::open_split_left`. `architect-1` read `openFile`'s doc comment while planning, but plan 1 named neither it nor the note. Review 1 §5 found all of them, and round 2 fixed them.
- **An unpaired backtick at the base made a README edit render wrongly.** Line 421 of `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md` held three bare backticks, each in the chord Ctrl+grave, at `4fdcd98`. `technical-writer-1` added a `:bd` span, the pairing moved, and prettier stripped two spaces. The markdown checker and prettier both passed, and only review 1 §5 caught it.
- **A word-budget cut dropped a fact again.** `technical-writer-1` paid for each addition to `crates/wolf-gnome/src/README.md` with cuts, and its `search_panel.rs` entry lost that `All` off means Markdown-only. Review 1 §5 named it and `technical-writer-2` restored it, cutting another word to stay at 250. This repeats [application-state-9](application-state-9.md).
- **`runtime/nvim/README.md` grew to 59,608 bytes and took five reads.** `architect-1` read it with `cat`, then in four `sed` ranges, two of them cut with `cut -c`. This is the finding of [app-state-fixes-2](app-state-fixes-2.md), come up again.
- **Three reds are timeouts, not their own assertions.** C4a, C20b and C21a fail in `common::probe_values` with "timed out after 10s waiting for nvim to write the probe file". `coder-1` could not explain why, and reviews 1 and 2 both left it open. The panic message names neither Neovim's current window nor its mode, so the next agent to see it starts from nothing.
- **Plan 1 told the coder that the GNOME smokes' falsifiers could not be run.** Plan 1 §4 said C25 and C26 were Linux-only and out of `coder-1`'s reach. The coder's definition has `regina on linux`, and `coder-1` ran all four reds through it. The architect's definition still lacks it, as [followups-3](followups-3.md) found. Nothing was reworked here.
- **Three gaps landed, all recorded in review 2 §8.** `do_bdelete` resolves names with `bufnr()`, which also finds unlisted buffers that `:bdelete` would refuse with `E94`. The jumplist walk has no test of its own, as above. `specs/terminal-panes.md` line 88 still says "the last window is refused", which no longer covers a lone terminal pane beside the drawer (C17), and the task did not open it.

## Recommendations

1. **Add the differential case that tells the jumplist walk from the fallback.** In `crates/wolf-core/tests/drawer_last_pane.rs`, add a case beside C28 using `bd_after_jumps`' two-core pattern with the keys review 2 §3 gives: `:e a.md`, `:e b.md`, `:e c.md`, `:e a.md`, then `:bd`. Neovim shows `c.md` and the number fallback shows `b.md`, so the case goes red with the walk removed. Today that deletion passes every test.
2. **Resolve `:bd` names among listed buffers only.** In `do_bdelete` in `runtime/nvim/init.lua`, check `buflisted` after `vim.fn.bufnr(arg)`, as review 2 §2 proposes, so `:bd <name>` of a shown unlisted buffer beside the drawer reports `E94` as Neovim does. Add the test with it in `drawer_last_pane.rs`.
3. **Open the `specs/terminal-panes.md` sentence on line 88.** A task that names it can say "the tab's last pane, where the drawer does not count", which `technical-writer-1` and both reviews proposed. Neither round could change it, because this task did not open it.
4. **Flag an unpaired backtick in the markdown checker.** In `tools/lint/markdown_check.sh`, report a line outside a fenced block whose backticks, after removing double-backtick spans and escaped `` \` ``, are odd in number. Only one such line exists in the repository today, in a table of `notes/feat-list.md`, so the rule is cheap to adopt. It would have flagged line 421 of `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md` before this task edited it.
5. **Describe a queue and a blocking call by their entry point, not by the actions that reach them.** The `pendingFileOpens` and `pending_file_opens` doc comments could say what the queue holds and link `specs/cli-and-file-open.md` §5.2. The `open_split_left` sentence of `notes/wolf-gnome/crash-and-stall-recovery.md` could name `PaneInner::run_pending_file_opens` as its caller. Adding the drawer route would then have left all four true, where here they cost review 1 two items and round 2 four edits.
6. **Name the state when `probe_values` times out.** In `crates/wolf-core/tests/common/mod.rs`, have the timeout panic include `core.read_snapshot().active_win`, which needs no typed command. C4a, C20b and C21a would then say what broke, and the cause both reviews left open could be found.
7. **Split `runtime/nvim/README.md` and the wolf-gnome READMEs.** This is recommendation 1 of [cli-open-2-4](cli-open-2-4.md) and 4 of [app-state-fixes-3](app-state-fixes-3.md), come up again. Here it cost `architect-1` five reads of the runtime README and lost a fact to a word-budget cut in `crates/wolf-gnome/src/README.md`.
8. **Give the architect `regina on linux`.** This is recommendation 1 of [followups-3](followups-3.md), come up again. Here plan 1 §4 told the coder it could not run four falsifiers that it then ran.
