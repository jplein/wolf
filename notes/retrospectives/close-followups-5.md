## What went well

- **Keeping every heading made the inbound links free.** Plan 1 D2 kept the exact text of every heading of the three files, so the anchors that code, `BUILD.bazel`, specs and about 30 `init.lua` comments cite all survived. Plan 1 K3 checked it with a `grep '^#'` and `comm`, and review 1 and review 2 re-ran it in one command each. Only four links needed re-pointing, and doc-lint's anchor check passed on the first gate run on both workers.
- **The lesson of [close-followups-4](close-followups-4.md) held.** Plan 1 D7 cited that report's 67 names lost to a whole-file `Write`, and K4 and K5 diffed the `wolf_*` and backticked names at the start against the result. `technical-writer-1` edited the three big files only through anchored string replacements that asserted one match each. Review 1 found no lost name.
- **`--entry-words` measured the entry budgets directly.** `./tools/lint/markdown_check.sh --entry-words` gave the `wolf_wait.rs` count to the plan, both writers and both reviewers, and found `wolf_setup_layout(chan)` at 249 of 250 words. No agent built its own counter, which [open-items-14](open-items-14.md) recorded twice.
- **Existing mirror headings took the moved rationale with no code change.** Every `##` heading of `notes/runtime/nvim/init.lua.md` is already linked from an `init.lua` comment, so plan 1 D1 could move text there without the coder adding a comment for doc-lint `notes-unlinked`.

## What did not go well

- **A true decision was deleted as false, because its call site has no comment.** `technical-writer-1` cut "so a restart reports it too" from `## Terminal panes` as false, after one `grep` that showed `reportedTerminalFocus` is never reset and the 12 lines before `resetAfterRestart()`. The reverse holds: `resetAfterRestart()` clears `activePane` and `terminalByPane`, then calls `syncTerminalFocus()` at `PaneContainerView.swift:1649`, which reports the loss (review 1 F1). Plan 1 M2 cut a note's rule wherever a site comment states it, but that call has none, so the note was its only statement.
- **The task's "Done means" was read two ways.** The instructions required `./gates.sh` on both workers. `architect-1` read `.claude/skills/regina/flow.yaml`, saw that `gate-implementation` follows only `coder`, and handed off to the writer, arguing in plan 1 §4 that the documentation gate covers every step a Markdown edit affects. `reviewer-1` read the line literally and rejected (F2). Plan 2 D8 then sent a coder with nothing to change, only to reach the gate.
- **What the round trip cost.** `architect-2` through `reviewer-2` added $4.27 and about 25 minutes. F2 accounts for the three coder invocations ($1.29), about 8 minutes of `coder-3` and one gate run on both workers. F1 accounts for most of `technical-writer-2`'s $0.58, plus the share of both reviews and plan 2 spent on it.
- **Two coders ended their turn while the gate ran, and were replaced.** `coder-1` started `./gates.sh` in the background and ended with "Waiting for the completion notification"; `coder-2` ended with "I'll pick this up when it exits". - **A macOS flake cost `coder-3` a second gate run and two diagnostic runs.** `//crates/wolf-core:terminal_session` failed in `resize_reaches_the_frame_and_the_shell`, where `stty size` printed `24 80` at `terminal_session.rs:138`. - **Two entries are at or near their budget after the cut.** `wolf_setup_layout(chan)` in `runtime/nvim/README.md` is at 249 of 250 words (review 1 §2, review 2 §8), and the README is at 47,994 bytes against plan 1 D4's 46,000 aim. The next change to that entry has no room.

## Recommendations

1. **Comment why `resetAfterRestart()` ends with `syncTerminalFocus()`.** At `crates/wolf-macos/Sources/WolfMacOS/PaneContainerView.swift:1649`, add a one-line comment that the call reports the focus loss of the session whose pane the restart dropped, which core needs for its rescan, linking `specs/terminal-panes.md#the-file-tree-rescan`. Without it the call looks dead once `terminalViews` is empty, and the note was its only statement (review 1 F1, $0.58 and a review round). Every later cut that uses plan 1's site-comment rule depends on such comments.
2. **Give a Markdown-only plan a route through the implementation gate, or let "Done means" name the documentation gate.** In `.claude/skills/regina/flow.yaml`, either let the architect hand off to a state that runs `gate-implementation` before `technical-writer`, or have the request template's "Done means" for a documentation-only task name the documentation gate rather than `./gates.sh`. Here the gap cost review 1's F2, three coder invocations ($1.29) and one more full gate run on both workers. [open-items-14](open-items-14.md) recorded the opposite cost, a no-op coder, on the same route.
3. **Say that ending a turn ends the invocation, and give the poll.** This is recommendation 4 of [application-state-6](application-state-6.md) and 3 of [close-commands-2-4](close-commands-2-4.md), come up a third time. In the paragraph "The Bash tool stops a foreground command after 10 minutes" of `.claude/skills/regina/agents/coder.md` and its siblings, say that nothing wakes the agent when a background command ends, and give `coder-3`'s loop of `[ -f "$d/exit" ]` and `sleep 5`, bounded under 10 minutes. Here two discrepancies cost $0.45 and two killed gate runs.
4. **List open flakes where planners read.** This is recommendation 1 of [fixes-1](fixes-1.md), 4 of [fixes-2](fixes-2.md) and 1 of [titlebar-colours-2](titlebar-colours-2.md), come up a fourth time. Start `notes/known-flakes.md` with `terminal_session`'s `resize_reaches_the_frame_and_the_shell`, its signature `24 80` at `terminal_session.rs:138` and its rate of about 1 in 5 on macOS under the whole binary. It would have saved `coder-3` its two diagnostic runs and its reading of `Session::resize`.
5. **Make room in `wolf_setup_layout(chan)` before the next change to it.** In `runtime/nvim/README.md`, cut that entry by the two-question test to about 220 words, as this task did for `wolf_wait.rs`. Both reviews named it, and the next task that touches the layout notification otherwise has to cut and add in the same pass.
