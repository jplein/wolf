# Retrospective: Zoom Pane on GNOME

Last verified at commit `82280c877aa26701c64ad0582c151c252fbca610`.

The task ported Zoom Pane to `wolf-gnome`. It added the pure seam `crates/wolf-gnome/src/pane_zoom.rs` and its fast table `tests/pane_zoom.rs`, the zoom branch of `rebuild_pane_area` in `pane_view.rs`, the palette row through `UiAction::ZoomPane`, and the cage smoke `smoke_headless/zoom_pane.rs`. Its commits are `ffecffd`, `ed89c7a` and `82280c8`. No gate failed, and nothing was resumed or flagged as a discrepancy.

It took two round trips. `coder-1` disputed one test assertion, and review 1 rejected one paragraph of the tests README.

The eleven invocations cost about $29.13. `architect-1` was $8.13 (78 turns, 20.8 minutes), `test-writer-1` $3.93, `coder-1` $4.07 (36 minutes), `technical-writer-1` $2.55 and `reviewer-1` $5.13. The two rework rounds added $1.96 and $3.36.

## What went well

- **A sibling seam and its macOS original made the rules cheap.** `pane_zoom.rs` follows the shape of `drawer.rs` and `other_pane_seam.rs`, and plan 1 ported `PaneZoom.swift` with `PaneZoomTests.swift` as its test table, K1 to K10 case for case. All 17 fast-tier falsifiers went red on their planned assertion the first time, as `coder-1` recorded.
- **The smoke helpers were reusable.** `zoom_pane.rs` took `pointer_command`, the `require_leg` pattern, `terminal::drawer_strip_edges`, `pointer::MIN_DRAG_CELLS` and `preview::PREVIEW_OTHER_PANE_QUERY` from its siblings, with `drawer_divider.rs` as its model. `coder-1`'s run before any code got through legs L to P, so both modelled drags landed on the first Linux run of a 762-line smoke.
- **A retrospective's finding reached the plan.** Plan 1 D1 decided to hold no `gtk::Paned` aside across the zoom, citing the dispose order that [pane-ui-2](pane-ui-2.md) recorded. No red came from it, and `technical-writer-1` wrote the reason into `notes/wolf-gnome/window-structure.md`, which carries out recommendation 1 of that report.
- **A zoom that is a rebuild left the unzoomed path untouched.** Because the unzoomed branch of `rebuild_pane_area` is the old code moved into an `else`, requirement 4 (`PANE_ORIGIN_Y_EXPECTED` and the pointer targets) needed no new test. The implementation gate passed on both workers on its only run.
- **Falsifier records with checksums and line numbers made the rework small.** `coder-1` recorded each red with the file's `sha256sum` and the panic line. So plan 2 D12 kept every line outside leg U in place, and `coder-2` re-ran only `U1` and `U2` ($0.61, 6 minutes) rather than the eleven smoke IDs `coder-1` had spent about half an hour on.

## What did not go well

- **The tests README's drag convention had been false since `2946f33`, and the technical writer never read the two reports that flagged it.** The paragraph "A divider handle cannot be modelled at all" under "Smoke conventions" in `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md` said every drag smoke bisects. `drawer_divider.rs` has modelled its press through `drawer_strip_edges().handle_y` since pane-ui-2's `2946f33`, and plan 1 chose a modelled press for `zoom_pane.rs` too.
- **Three agents saw the contradiction, and the plan scheduled no fix.** `architect-1`'s transcript shows it read the paragraph, but plan 1 §5 asked only that the new entry mention "the modelled handle presses". `test-writer-1` named the contradiction as the technical writer's to reconcile, and `coder-1` listed it under "Documents this change makes wrong". `technical-writer-1` listed the reports directory in its first command and opened neither report.
- **That cost the second round trip.** Review 1 found it as `R1`, with the missing zoomed-drawer gap as `R2`. `architect-3`, `technical-writer-2` and `reviewer-2` cost $3.36 and a second documentation gate run, for a change of four lines added and one removed. The technical writer's definition, as its system prompt shows, names the plan and `git diff` as its inputs and never a coder's or test writer's report. [round-3-10](round-3-10.md) found the same thing.
- **Plan 1 predicted the wrong red for `U2`, and only a falsifier run could show it.** Plan 1 §4 said resetting the drawer share would fail leg U on D's height. The drawer's paned holds the whole editor tree above it, as `notes/wolf-gnome/window-structure.md` says, so A's height moved too, and the leg checked A's whole `(w, h)` first under a message blaming the tree weights.
- **The misattribution survived to the coder.** `test-writer-1` wrote the plan's order and, by its definition, ran no test. `coder-1` found the misattribution, and `architect-2`, `test-writer-2` and `coder-2` cost $1.96 to move one assertion. Nothing in the repository invited this, so it has no recommendation, and the falsifier procedure worked as intended.
- **The Linux dev shell still has no `perl` or `python3`.** `coder-1` reported that each remote disabling edit had to be a patch it built locally with Python's `difflib` and sent through a heredoc to `git apply`, for 28 falsifier IDs. That is recommendation 2 of [round-3-3](round-3-3.md), come up again.
- **The command counts in `notes/feat-list.md` were three commands stale.** The `drain_commands()` paragraph said "Of the 28 commands, 24 dispatch" when `ui_action_for` had 31 arms. pane-ui-3 and pane-ui-4 added commands without editing it, and `technical-writer-1` corrected it to 31 and 27. That is [round-3-11](round-3-11.md) recommendation 7 on prose counts, which [pane-ui-3](pane-ui-3.md) recommendation 1 carried to test constants, come up again.
- **The wolf-gnome documents are at their limits again.** The `pane_view.rs` entry of `crates/wolf-gnome/src/README.md` was at 250 of 250 words, and `technical-writer-1` reworded its other bullets to fit the zoom. `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md` went from 112,243 to 115,798 bytes. `pane_view.rs` is 5,682 lines, and `architect-1` read about 2,200 of them in nine ranges.
- **The task instructions pointed at notes that do not hold what they said.** They named a `max-position` snap-back in `notes/wolf-gnome/pane-weights-and-dividers.md` and a list of commands without a GNOME equivalent in `notes/wolf-gnome-decisions.md`. Neither is there: pane-ui-2's `a35962d` removed the snap-back wording from the notes, and the list is in `notes/feat-list.md`. `architect-1` searched three times for the snap-back, and plan 1 D10 corrected the second. These are the request's instructions, not the repository, so nothing is recommended for them.
- **Two small defects landed, both noted by review 2 and left.** The `apply_zoom_event` doc comment at `crates/wolf-gnome/src/pane_view.rs:1546` says "every caller rebuilds the pane area after it", a claim about callers that `CLAUDE.md`'s comment rule steers away from. The rewritten bisection paragraph in the tests README says each probe is preceded by a click "at the low seed", where `bisect_divider` clicks at the search's current low bound.

## Recommendations

1. **Name the coder's and test writer's reports as the technical writer's input.** This is recommendation 1 of [round-3-10](round-3-10.md), come up again. Under `reason: zero` in `.claude/skills/regina/agents/technical-writer.md`, name the latest `reports/coder-<N>.md` section "Documents this change makes wrong" and the test writer's deviations as documents to check. Here both reports named the false paragraph, and reading them would have saved `architect-3`, `technical-writer-2`, `reviewer-2` and a documentation gate run, $3.36.
2. **Have the plan's documentation section name every convention the plan departs from.** In `.claude/skills/regina/agents/architect.md`, ask that §5 name each documented convention whose rule the plan's code or tests break, with the paragraph to rewrite. Plan 1 §4 chose a modelled press that the "Smoke conventions" paragraph forbade, which `architect-1` had read, and §5 asked only for a mention in the new entry. With this rule, `technical-writer-1` would have had the edit in its plan even without the reports.
3. **Drop the totals from the `drain_commands()` paragraph of `notes/feat-list.md`.** List only the exceptions by name, `HideApplication` and the deferred commands, and let `ui_action_for` and `EXPECTED_DISPATCHED_IDS` hold the counts. This is [round-3-11](round-3-11.md) recommendation 7 come up again, here stale through two tasks and found only because `technical-writer-1` recounted the match arms.
4. **Add `perl` and `python3` to `linuxShell` in `flake.nix`.** This is [round-3-3](round-3-3.md) recommendation 2, come up again. `coder-1` would have applied 28 disabling edits in the Linux snapshot directly, rather than building each as a patch on macOS.
5. **Split the wolf-gnome READMEs and `pane_view.rs`.** These are recommendation 7 of [nerd-font-3](nerd-font-3.md) and recommendation 3 of [pane-ui-2](pane-ui-2.md), come up again. A zoom and drawer submodule of `pane_view.rs` with its own entry would have held this task's 145 changed lines and freed the `pane_view.rs` entry's budget. A tests README split by topic, its smoke conventions apart from the per-file entries, would have put the false paragraph in a short file that agents read in full.
6. **Mend review 2's two leftovers.** Change "every caller rebuilds" to "the caller rebuilds" in the `apply_zoom_event` doc comment, and "at the low seed" to "at the current low bound" in the bisection paragraph of `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md`.
