# Retrospective: Close Window and Quit refuse while a tab holds unsaved changes

Last verified at commit `0ec0e5716d9b399520398eeddb070ea581aff417`.

The task made Close Window, the window's close button and Quit ask every tab they would close, on both platforms, through pure seams in `crates/wolf-gnome/src/tabs_ui.rs` and `crates/wolf-macos/Sources/WolfKeyMap/WindowCloseSeam.swift`. Its commits are `3fc2613` (code, spec and tests) and `0ec0e57` (docs). It took nine invocations, about $37.90 and 116 minutes of agent time: `architect-1` $10.09, `test-writer-1` $5.85, `coder-1` $6.00, `architect-2` $1.18, `test-writer-2` $0.48, `coder-2` $0.68, `coder-3` $0.71, `technical-writer-1` $7.54 and `reviewer-1` $5.37.

## What went well

- **`coder-1` measured the fix before it rejected.** It ran CW20 in a remote snapshot with `[Untitled.md]` in place of `[]`, saw it pass and saw CW20a, b and d go red. It also named the false comment in `restore.rs` that misled plan 1. So `architect-2` settled both disputes in 21 turns for $1.18, and `test-writer-2` made the three edits in 12 turns for $0.48.
- **A named constant held the value the premise needed.** `wolf_gnome::save_ui::UNTITLED_SAVE_NAME` already existed, and `header_bar.rs`'s `HB_LAUNCH_BUFFER` already relied on it, so plan 2 §4 could point CW20 at the constant and not at a literal.
- **Regina's command logs outlived a lost invocation.** `coder-2` wrote no output document, but its remote requests 1 to 7 stayed under the task's command log directory. `coder-3` cited those six falsifier reds verbatim instead of rerunning them, and review 1 §4 checked them against the logs word for word.
- **The existing session commit test covered the new close path.** CW21 is `SessionSeamTests.testATabGroupClosedInOneTurnKeepsEveryTab`, unchanged. The close-burst commit in `notes/wolf-macos-decisions.md` made the tab-group close one more caller of a tested seam.
- **Plan 1 counted the entry budgets.** Its documentation item listed ten entries at or near 250 words with their counts, as [close-commands-2-2](close-commands-2-2.md) recommendation 2 asks, and `technical-writer-1` finished every entry at or under the cap. As in [close-commands-2-3](close-commands-2-3.md), the habit works when the plan states it.
- **Both gates passed on their first run on both workers.** No `FLUSH_TIMEOUT` flake showed this time, so the gate cost one run each.

## What did not go well

- **The one round trip had two causes, and either alone would have forced it.** `coder-1` returned `rejected` on two defects in test files it may not write. The round cost `architect-2`, `test-writer-2`, `coder-2` and `coder-3`, $3.05 and about 23 minutes, though some of `coder-3`'s Linux gate run was owed anyway.
- **The first cause was a clippy lint the test writer could not see.** `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/tab_close.rs:327` tripped `clippy::type_complexity` under the gate's `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`. The file names `Quit` and `WindowClose`, which do not exist at `6b124f3`, so it could not compile before the coder. `test-writer-1` ran clippy on `--test smoke_headless` only, and this is the pattern of `coder-1` in [ghostty-5](ghostty-5.md), come up again.
- **The test writer definition gives two clippy forms, neither of them the gate's.** Its Linux paragraph suggests `cargo clippy -p wolf-gnome --all-targets`, and its compile paragraph `cargo clippy -p wolf-gnome --tests`. It says nothing of stubbing the plan's API in the remote snapshot, which `test-writer-1` did in [application-state-4](application-state-4.md), [application-state-5](application-state-5.md) and [ghostty-4](ghostty-4.md), and which would have compiled `tab_close.rs`.
- **The second cause was a premise copied from two comments nothing asserted.** Plan 1 §5 said CW20's window 2 has a buffer "whose buffer is unnamed". It came from the `TWOWIN_READBACK_B_NAME` doc and a shell comment in `smoke_headless/restore.rs`, while that smoke only checks that the name is none of window A's files. `test-writer-1` listed the premise under Assumptions, and only `coder-1`'s cage run tested it. This is recommendation 2 of [application-state-1](application-state-1.md), come up again, as [application-state-6](application-state-6.md) recommendation 2 already noted.
- **`coder-2` ended its turn while both gates ran, and was replaced.** It started the macOS gate and `regina on linux -- ./gates.sh` in the background, wrote "I'll pick up again when they exit" and ended. Nothing wakes an agent then, so the run ended with no output document, the background jobs were killed, and `coder-3` ($0.71, 15 minutes) reran both gates. This is recommendation 4 of [application-state-6](application-state-6.md), come up again: the coder definition still says only "poll it".
- **The whole Linux gate through `regina on` sits near its 10-minute limit.** `coder-3` reports that `./gates.sh` there comes close to 600 seconds, and it ran it under `timeout 585` to keep the step summary. The coder definition names neither the limit's effect on the whole gate nor that form.
- **Every documented file was at its budget or its ceiling before the change.** Ten entries started at 242 to 250 words, so `technical-writer-1` cut before each addition, and it took 125 turns and $7.54. `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md` grew from 147,043 to 149,308 bytes and `notes/wolf-macos-decisions.md` from 54,810 to 57,786, against the 50,000-byte `big-file` warning. This is the finding of [regina-land-linux-titlebar](regina-land-linux-titlebar.md), [regina-land-toast](regina-land-toast.md) and [application-state-6](application-state-6.md), come up again.
- **Review 1 §5 left three imprecise docs in the landed work.** `crates/wolf-gnome/src/palette.rs:480-481` and `crates/wolf-gnome/src/README.md:601` say a dispatched Close Window tears the window down, which the check may now refuse. The fourth paragraph of "Closing a window and quitting ask every tab" in `notes/wolf-gnome-decisions.md` gives the drain's reason for the last page's confirm, not the arm's own. `notes/wolf-ffi-decisions.md#discriminant-tables-are-append-only` lists the modules by hand and omits `close_tab_answer` and `unsaved_answer`.
- **Review 1 §3 found the macOS glue untested.** `closeGroup` with live controllers, `applicationShouldTerminate` and `windowShouldClose`'s group branch run only on screen, which the instructions accepted. Nothing fails if the confirm in `on_close_page`'s `CloseWindow` arm, `crates/wolf-gnome/src/tab_view.rs:806-809`, is removed.

## Recommendations

1. **Have the test writer type-check against a stub of the plan's API, with the gate's clippy.** In the "Compile what can compile" paragraph of `.claude/skills/regina/agents/test-writer.md`, say to add `todo!()` stubs of the plan's §3 signatures inside the `regina on linux -- sh -c` command and run `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` over them. Replace the two clippy forms the definition gives with that one. It would have caught `tab_close.rs:327` here and the patterns of [ghostty-5](ghostty-5.md), and it is the method three earlier tasks found by themselves.
2. **Make the architect mark a premise taken from a comment.** This is recommendation 2 of [application-state-1](application-state-1.md), come up again. In `.claude/skills/regina/agents/architect.md`, say that a value a planned test expects is cited from an assertion or a named constant, such as `UNTITLED_SAVE_NAME`, or else is marked unmeasured for the coder to confirm first. Together with recommendation 1, it would have saved the $3.05 round.
3. **Say that ending a turn ends the invocation, and give the poll and the Linux gate form.** This is recommendation 4 of [application-state-6](application-state-6.md), come up again. In the definitions' paragraph "The Bash tool stops a foreground command after 10 minutes", add that nothing wakes the agent when a background command ends, and give a foreground `until grep -q '^exit ' <log>; do sleep 20; done`. In `.claude/skills/regina/agents/coder.md`, give `coder-3`'s `timeout 585 ./gates.sh > <log> 2>&1` as the whole Linux gate. It would have saved `coder-3`.
4. **Fix the docs review 1 §5 left.** Write "may tear the window down" at `crates/wolf-gnome/src/palette.rs:480-481` and `crates/wolf-gnome/src/README.md:601`. Give the arm's own reason, sparing a `NoAnswer` tab a second ask, in the fourth paragraph of "Closing a window and quitting ask every tab" in `notes/wolf-gnome-decisions.md`. In `notes/wolf-ffi-decisions.md#discriminant-tables-are-append-only`, name no modules, so the next table cannot make the list stale.
5. **Split the GNOME READMEs and `notes/wolf-macos-decisions.md` before the next close or quit task.** This is the finding of [regina-land-linux-titlebar](regina-land-linux-titlebar.md) and [application-state-6](application-state-6.md), come up again. Here it cost most of `technical-writer-1`'s 125 turns, since every entry it touched had to be cut before it grew.
