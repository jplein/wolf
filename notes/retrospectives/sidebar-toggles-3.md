# Retrospective: case and regex toggles in the GNOME search panel

Last verified at commit `da50382f66d95bcaed08c6c8e5881fe5b0a62f7f`.

The task added `Aa` and `.*` toggles under the query field of `crates/wolf-gnome/src/search_panel.rs`, made Enter re-run when they change, and showed rg's error exit in the status line. Its commits are `97db711` (code and tests) and `da50382` (documentation). Eight invocations cost $22.30 over about 74 minutes. `test-writer-1` ($6.09, 59 turns) and `architect-1` ($5.80, 61 turns, 17 minutes) were the largest, then `reviewer-1` ($3.43) and `coder-1` ($2.46).

There was one round trip: `coder-1` disputed one OCR assertion, which plan 2 settled. Both gates passed on their first run on both workers, and review 1 approved with minor notes.

## What went well

- **The pure seams carried most of the falsifiers.** `search_options_for`, `enter_action` and `search_status` take the toggles as values, so `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/sidebar_search.rs` covers all four toggle states, the Enter rule and the stale and error arms with no display. `coder-1` ran C1a to C5a against them in one scripted loop, each red and restored by checksum.
- **The feature-split smoke harness took a new module cheaply again.** `smoke_headless/search_toggles.rs` needed one `mod` line in `main.rs`, and reused `projsearch_sidebar_band` for its OCR. This is the same finding as [sidebar-toggles-2](sidebar-toggles-2.md), so the split of [round-3-11](round-3-11.md) recommendation 5 keeps paying.
- **The fixture guards ruled out false greens.** `assert_search_toggle_fixtures` and `fixture_guards.rs`'s filler guard check that no file, line or query holds a status term or result word. Review 1 §3 relied on them, and plan 2 relied on the guard to check its replacement term mechanically.
- **The retained band crop made the OCR failure diagnosable in one run.** The crop is kept as a file for exactly this, as the doc of `ocr_band_to_lowercase` in `main.rs` says. `coder-1` looked at it and could tell a drawn status line from a misread one, so the dispute was precise and plan 2 needed no new measurement.
- **libadwaita was read from the pinned build, not the web.** `test-writer-1` extracted the Adwaita stylesheet from the Linux worker's `libadwaita-1.so` with `gresource extract`, and measured label widths with `pango-view`, through `regina on linux`. [sidebar-toggles-2](sidebar-toggles-2.md) saw `architect-1` read `main` from the web instead; this is a working answer to its recommendation 3.

## What did not go well

- **One OCR term was misread, and fixing it took three invocations.** S3 asserted that capture 0 holds `failed`, from `Search failed: rg exited with code 2`. Tesseract read `failed:` as `faile` on every run while reading `exited` correctly. `coder-1` returned `rejected` without running C8a to C8c or `./gates.sh`, and `architect-2` ($1.53), `test-writer-2` ($0.49) and `coder-2` ($0.86) followed, about 14 minutes.
- **The plan's contingency pointed at the wrong remedy.** Plan 1 foresaw that the dim status label might not OCR, since no smoke had read it, and prescribed a new reader that scales the crop up. Plan 2 rejected that as unmeasured machinery and followed the crate's precedent instead, `CONFIRM_OCR_OPTIONS = "ancel"` in `smoke_headless/editor.rs`: assert a word or fragment the reader does read. That precedent lives only in a test file, and `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md` § Smoke conventions says nothing about choosing a term.
- **Nobody measured the new term before the code existed.** `test-writer-1` rendered `Aa`, `.*` and `No matches` with `pango-view` for widths, but ran no `tesseract` on any status text. A render of the error line at the sidebar's width would likely have shown the misread before `coder-1` ran. The round trip was otherwise structural: the coder may not edit a test file, so any misread term costs a test writer invocation.
- **Falsifier predictions were wrong again before any run.** Plan 1 predicted C3b's red at case (c) and C6b's at assertion 3; they went red at case (a) and assertion 2. Plan 2 §4 recorded both, and `test-writer-2` fixed the C6b row and C8b's edit in `search_toggles.rs`. This is [ghostty-5](ghostty-5.md) recommendation 2, come up again; here it rode along in a round the OCR dispute forced.
- **A coverage gap in the tests README went false and landed.** `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md` still says "The search panel's status line reaches no assertion" and that no capture shows a Pango ellipsis. S2 now reads `changed` and `no matches` from the status line, and S3 reads `exited` from an ellipsized one. `technical-writer-1` added the `search_toggles.rs` entry without rewriting that bullet, and review 1 §5 did not catch it.
- **The wolf-gnome READMEs are at their limits.** The `search_panel.rs` entry in `crates/wolf-gnome/src/README.md` had 249 of its 250 words, so `technical-writer-1` cut its seams list to a pointer to the module doc (review 1 §8). `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md` grew by 1,088 bytes to 107,810, and `test-writer-1` printed the whole of it in one `cat`. This is [sidebar-toggles-2](sidebar-toggles-2.md) recommendation 4, which repeats [regina-land-linux-titlebar](regina-land-linux-titlebar.md) and [ghostty-4](ghostty-4.md).
- **A capture still has to be copied back by hand.** `coder-1` base64-encoded the crop through `regina on linux` stdout and decoded it on the Mac, after one attempt that lost the file because a worker's temp directory does not survive between commands. It cost three commands here; [regina-land-terminfo-2](regina-land-terminfo-2.md) saw the same workaround.
- **Two doc lines were left unwrapped.** The seams line of `search_panel.rs`'s module doc (about line 105) and `smoke_headless/sidebar.rs:68` run to 109 and 119 characters (review 1 §8). Doc comments are exempt from the width check, so nothing caught it.

## Recommendations

1. **Write the OCR term rules into § Smoke conventions.** In `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md`, say that a term from a label no smoke has read is measured first with `tesseract --psm 11` on a render of the real string. When a drawn word misreads, assert another word or fragment that reads, as `CONFIRM_OCR_OPTIONS` and `ST_OCR_RG_EXITED` do, and never add a reader or restyle the label. Add [round-3-11](round-3-11.md) recommendation 4's accent-selection fact, still missing. Plan 1 would then have planned `exited`, saving `architect-2` and `test-writer-2`.
2. **Rewrite the status-line gap bullet in `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md`.** Say what still reaches no assertion, the `WOLF_RG_BIN unset` arm, the windowing of a row to `char_budget`, a row's Pango ellipsis and the re-windowing after a divider drag, and drop the claim that no smoke reads the status line.
3. **Split the two wolf-gnome READMEs.** This is [sidebar-toggles-2](sidebar-toggles-2.md) recommendation 4, come up again. The `search_panel.rs` entry has no room left for its next change, and `architect-1`, `test-writer-1` and `technical-writer-1` each read in the 107 KB tests README.
4. **Rewrap the two long doc lines** in `crates/wolf-gnome/src/search_panel.rs` and `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/smoke_headless/sidebar.rs` that review 1 §8 names.
