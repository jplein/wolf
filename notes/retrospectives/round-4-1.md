# Retrospective: Neovim panes vanish while a terminal pane is open

Last verified at commit `633694e63211caa36f7b944864c0949c8b04d6b0`.

The task fixed a reported bug: Neovim panes went blank while a terminal pane was open. On macOS, `TerminalPaneView.draw(_:)` fills its dirty rect, and under the SDK's default `clipsToBounds == false` that fill covered every pane in the container. The fix is `clipsToBounds = true` in `TerminalPaneView.init`.

Linux was shown not to have the bug, by a new cage smoke that was green on the base commit. The task's commits are `c9da7ed` (code and tests) and `633694e` (documentation).

There was no round trip. Five invocations ran once each, both gates passed on both workers on their first run, and review 1 approved with minor notes. The task cost about $20.08: `architect-1` $8.50 (102 turns, 19 minutes), `test-writer-1` $6.94 (85 turns, 14 minutes), `reviewer-1` $1.97, `coder-1` $1.60 and `technical-writer-1` $1.07.

## What went well

- **The macOS offscreen renderer made the pixel tests cheap to write and to run.** `TerminalViewFixture` already started a real Neovim and shell, and `TerminalDrawerTests` already had a crimson-count check that plan 1 told the test writer to copy. `coder-1` got T1a, T2a and T3a red on its first base-commit run, then ran three falsifiers, for $1.60 in 49 turns. Keep the fixture and the renderer as the way to test what macOS draws.
- **`regina on linux` let the coder prove a negative on the other platform.** `coder-1` ran the new smoke G1 on the base commit, then under the falsifier F4, through four `regina on linux` commands, without a gate round. That is what let the report say plainly that Linux does not show the bug.
- **The smoke guard tables kept G1 from being vacuous.** `ASSERTED_OCR_TERMS`, `RELAUNCH_FIXTURE_NAMES` and `restore_fixture_names_cannot_satisfy_their_own_assertions` made the test writer show that the marker word `ZZNEARPANE` occurs only in the fixture. Review 1 §3 relied on them to rule out a false green.

## What did not go well

- **The base README described a clip the code did not do.** The `TerminalPaneView.swift` entry of `crates/wolf-macos/Sources/WolfMacOS/README.md` said "a frame larger than the view is clipped", which was false until this fix, and no test pinned it. `architect-1` found the cause only from the macOS 14 AppKit release notes on the web, and spent most of its cost reading `PaneContainerView.swift` (3,103 lines), core and the GTK draw path in slices. The rule now sits in `notes/wolf-macos-decisions.md` § Terminal panes.
- **`smoke_headless.rs` cost the test writer most of its turns.** 35 of the 81 shell commands of `test-writer-1` read or searched the 39,100-line file, from line 1 to line 33,600, for the drawer smoke, the native-tabs smoke, the pointer harness and the guard tables. Its diff has 26 hunks, from line 11 to line 33,679. This is recommendation 5 of [round-3-11](round-3-11.md), 2 of [ghostty-4](ghostty-4.md) and 4 of [ghostty-5](ghostty-5.md), come up a fourth time.
- **Most of that diff was hand-kept counts, under a rule that forbids them.** About 18 of the 26 hunks only change a count, such as "sixty-four" to "sixty-five" or `78 passed` to `79 passed`, and `technical-writer-1` changed the same counts in `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md`. The base commit `372aeb3` had just added the `CLAUDE.md` rule that no comment counts the tests that use something, yet plan 1 §3 asked for the update, and review 1 §5 flagged it. This is recommendation 7 of [round-3-11](round-3-11.md), 3 of [ghostty-4](ghostty-4.md) and 5 of [ghostty-5](ghostty-5.md), come up again.
- **A wolf-core test flaked in the coder's gate run.** `coder-1`'s first macOS `./gates.sh` failed only in `open_existing_markdown` of `//crates/wolf-core:save_open`, with `heading cell hl_id (0) should differ from body cell hl_id (0)`, and cost a ten-run check and a second full gate run. The test reads one snapshot once `start_core_with_temp_md` returns, and [retro-fixes-3](retro-fixes-3.md) made that helper wait for the drawn file, not for treesitter's highlights. It is the third startup flake in these reports, after [open-items-12](open-items-12.md) and [open-items-14](open-items-14.md).
- **Plan 1 named a tab-switch chord that does not exist.** It told G1 to switch tabs with "the chord the native-tabs smoke uses", but that smoke clicks the tab bar, and `runtime/nvim/init.lua` maps `gt`, `gT` and the rest to `<Nop>`. `test-writer-1` found this and made G1 a pointer smoke at the cost of a few searches, and no round trip. The fact is in the code and the tests README, so nothing in the repository invited the slip.
- **Two small defects landed in G1, as review 1 noted.** Its failure message claims that the close legs g2, g4 and g7 detect stale paint where the terminal was. They cannot, because they OCR the whole page and the rest of the editor still holds the word. Its doc comment also holds a sentence of history: "The macOS front-end's `TerminalPaneView` did the first".
- **The macOS worker still cannot format wolf-gnome.** `test-writer-1`'s first `regina on linux -- cargo fmt --all -- --check` failed, and it applied the printed diff by hand before a second run passed. This is recommendation 1 of [ghostty-4](ghostty-4.md), come up again.

## Recommendations

1. **Drop the hand-kept smoke counts.** This is [ghostty-5](ghostty-5.md) recommendation 5, come up a fourth time, and since `372aeb3` the counts break a `CLAUDE.md` rule. Remove every count of smokes, OCR smokes and pointer smokes, and the `NN passed` line, from the comments of `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/smoke_headless.rs` and from `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md` § `smoke_headless.rs` and § Pointer injection. About 18 of this task's 26 hunks, and the technical writer's whole edit of that README, would not have existed.
2. **Split `smoke_headless.rs` by feature.** This is recommendation 5 of [round-3-11](round-3-11.md), come up a fourth time. A `terminal` module holding the drawer, split and tab smokes, `pin_terminal_smoke_shell` and their constants, beside a shared module for the guard tables, would have spared `test-writer-1` most of its 35 searches of a 39,100-line file.
3. **Make `open_existing_markdown` wait for the highlights.** In `crates/wolf-core/tests/save_open.rs`, poll `read_snapshot()` with a deadline until the heading and body cells' `hl_id`s differ, and fail with the last values only after the deadline. `coder-1`'s failure and its ten passing runs alone are the evidence. It would have saved one full macOS `./gates.sh` run in this task, and the next one that hits it.
4. **Fix G1's two review notes.** In `neovim_panes_stay_visible_beside_terminals_under_cage`, narrow the close legs' line of the failure message to "the editor is not painted after the close", and cut the history sentence from its doc comment. A later reader would otherwise trust the close legs for a case only the macOS T1b, T2b and T3c check.
