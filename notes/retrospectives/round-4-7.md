# Add falsifier IDs, spec sentences and fail-fast ssh options to the regina harness

Last verified at commit `82f1ddb55f51429d37f602db29ee188df4de44ed`.

The task edited `.claude/skills/regina/`: one falsifier ID per test in the architect, coder and reviewer definitions, spec sentences in the architect's documentation list, and an `ssh` line for `[worker wolf linux]` in `regina.conf.template`. It ran straight through. `architect-1`, `coder-1`, `technical-writer-1` and `reviewer-1` each ran once, both gates passed on both workers on their first run, and review 1 approved with three minor notes. There was no rejection, non-zero gate, discrepancy or resume.

The four invocations cost about $4.94 and 22 minutes. `architect-1` was the largest at $2.01, 40 turns and 7.8 minutes, then `reviewer-1` at $1.23, `technical-writer-1` at $1.01 and `coder-1` at $0.69.

## What went well

- **The measurement plan made the `ssh` decision checkable without a test target.** Plan 1 §4 named a silent `nc -d -k -l` listener and three runs. `coder-1` recorded M1 and M3 still waiting at the 120 s cap and M2 failing in 30.00 s with "Connection timed out during banner exchange", and review 1 confirmed each number in the transcript. The Seatbelt worker allowed the listener on `127.0.0.1`, as plan 1 §1 predicted from regina's `network = unrestricted` profile.
- **Plan 1 handed on the store path of regina's docs.** It named one copy of `crates/regina-cli/README.md` under `/nix/store` in §1, so `coder-1` and `technical-writer-1` read the `ssh` key's rules and regina's `DECISIONS.md` there directly, without a search of their own.
- **`--entry-words` gave the writer the margin directly.** `technical-writer-1` measured both README entries with `markdown_check.sh --entry-words` and ended at 249 of 250 words in each, with no counter script of its own. This is the use [round-4-6](round-4-6.md) recorded.

## What did not go well

- **The README sends agents to a regina checkout no worker can read.** `.claude/skills/regina/README.md` line 20 names `~/dev/jplein/regina`. In a worker, `~` is the task's home, and `/Users/jplein/dev/jplein/regina` gave `Operation not permitted` under Seatbelt. `architect-1` then spent about eight commands grepping every `*-source/crates/regina-cli/README.md` in `/nix/store` for the `ssh` key and picking one copy. `reviewer-1` hit the same two failures and ran three loops over the store copies to show that all 56 copies past the `7b72b1e` floor document the key.
- **The `regina` README entries had no words to spare.** Plan 1 §5 counted the `regina.conf.template` and `agents/` entries at 245 and 231 words, which the checker measured as 243 and 229, and said the existing bullets "stay as they are". The new `ssh` bullet alone took about 70 words, so `technical-writer-1` reworded every other bullet of both entries, and review 1 accepted the deviation. The `timeout = 3600` bullet lost "an `interrupted` attempt" in the cut. [round-3-6](round-3-6.md) found the `agents/` entry at 249 words, so this came up again.
- **Nothing gated the five changed files, and base findings in them persist.** As in [round-3-6](round-3-6.md), `.prettierignore`, `tools/lint/markdown_all.sh` and doc-lint skip `.claude/`, so both gates passed without reading any of them. `coder-1` ran `markdown_check.sh` by hand and found three findings already at the base: "no longer" and "now" in `agents/architect.md`, and a 931-character "Pinning a crate…" paragraph in `agents/coder.md`. Both left them, as lines the task did not touch.
- **The preflight `ssh` commands still have no timeout.** Review 1 note 1 found that `SKILL.md:34-35` runs `ssh -o BatchMode=yes nixos.local …` without `ConnectTimeout`. Against a host that accepts and sends no banner, the preflight hangs the way the requests in the last run did, before the template's new line is ever used. The task opened only the template, so this is a gap left, not a defect.
- **`coder-1` ran `./gates.sh` twice.** Its first run was started with a shell `&` and redirected to a file, which recorded no exit status, so it ran the gate again in the foreground. The warm output root made the second run cheap, and nothing in the repository invited it.

## Recommendations

1. **Give agents a readable copy of regina's docs.** In `.claude/skills/regina/README.md`, say how a worker reaches regina's `crates/regina-cli/README.md` and `crates/regina-core/DECISIONS.md`, for example by having `regina-run.sh start` copy them into the run's state, or by naming the store path of the pinned regina in the template. `architect-1` and `reviewer-1` each lost about eight commands to `~/dev/jplein/regina` and a `/nix/store` search, and every task that changes the template or a definition will do the same.
2. **Gate formatting and the Markdown rules under `.claude/`.** This is [round-3-6](round-3-6.md) recommendation 1 and [round-3-4](round-3-4.md) recommendation 4, come up again. Run `prettier --ignore-path /dev/null --check` and `markdown_check.sh` on `.claude/skills/regina/` in both sides of `gates.sh`, and fix the three base findings in `agents/architect.md` and `agents/coder.md` in the same change. This task's done criterion was again a hand check that both gates could not prove.
3. **Split the `regina.conf.template` and `agents/` entries of `.claude/skills/regina/README.md`.** Move the line-level reasons, such as the `ssh` values and the measurements, into a mirror `notes/.claude/skills/regina/regina.conf.template.md` and link it from the template's comment, or move the `/orchestrate` sync rules to their own note. Both entries end this task at 249 of 250 words, so the next bullet in either costs a rewrite of the others, as it did here.
4. **Add `-o ConnectTimeout=30` to the preflight in `SKILL.md`.** Steps at `SKILL.md:34-35` would then fail a silent `nixos.local` in 30 s, as review 1 note 1 suggests, rather than hanging before `start`.
