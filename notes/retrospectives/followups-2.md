# Retrospective: derive the command registry's counts

Last verified at commit `cfa98b03fad06477dba7ea0aba972d8a60340e95`.

The task removed every hand-kept copy of the command registry's length, last discriminant and discriminant ranges, so appending a command bumps no number and renames no test. It carries out recommendations 1 and 2 of [pane-ui-3](pane-ui-3.md) and recommendation 1 of [pane-ui-4](pane-ui-4.md). Its commits are `1c8e4f4` and `7407c2e` (round 1) and `ca9a03b` and `cfa98b0` (round 2); `bd1fe82`, `da40d14` and `5b68544` in the same range are [followups-1](followups-1.md)'s.

It took two rounds. Review 1 rejected with two must-fix items and one recommendation, plan 2 accepted all three, and review 2 approved. No gate failed and there was no discrepancy or resume. The ten invocations cost about $20.41: round 1 $16.52 (`architect-1` $5.77 in 71 turns and 18 minutes, `coder-1` $3.78, `reviewer-1` $3.50, `technical-writer-1` $1.78, `test-writer-1` $1.69) and round 2 $3.89.

## What went well

- **The `declare_commands!` macro lets a table test see every variant.** Stable Rust cannot count an enum's variants, so plan 1 D2 wraps `Command` in a `macro_rules!` in `crates/wolf-core/src/command.rs` that emits `EXPECTED_COMMAND_COUNT` from the variant list. One test, `every_variant_has_one_row_at_its_discriminant`, replaced 56 per-command test functions. Plan 1 D2 says not to unwrap the macro into a plain `enum`; a cleanup round should keep it.
- **One pinned record per side made appends free and moves fatal.** `mod command_kind` in `crates/wolf-ffi/src/lib.rs` and the arms of `CommandKind.init?(ffi:)` are the only discriminant lists left, and `//crates/wolf-ffi:commands` and `CommandSeamTests` check them against the registry. `coder-1`'s check V1 appended a probe command to the three records alone, and all five affected targets stayed green; the 21 falsifiers each turned a test red.
- **The task listed every copy, measured by earlier reports.** The instructions named each site that pane-ui-4 edited for Zoom Pane, from the two earlier retrospectives. Review 1 checked every listed copy against the tree and found them all gone. The one copy the list missed is below.
- **`regina on linux` let one invocation record both platforms' reds.** `coder-1` ran the GNOME falsifiers C8a, C8b and C9a on the Linux worker from the Mac, and review 1 matched them against those command logs without a rerun.

## What did not go well

- **A copied constant outlived its original (review 1 R1).** `crates/wolf-ffi/tests/full_width.rs` held `const CMD_KIND_FULL_WIDTH: u8 = 22` and a poll helper, each documented as a copy from `commands.rs`; round 1 deleted both originals, leaving two false comments. `architect-1`, `test-writer-1` and `coder-1` each swept the tree, but for lengths, last discriminants and ranges, as the instructions framed it. 22 is a middle discriminant, and only `reviewer-1` found it. It cost `test-writer-2` ($0.49) and most of `architect-2` ($0.92) and `reviewer-2` ($1.19).
- **That copy is one of many in `crates/wolf-ffi/tests/`.** Twelve of its test files carry "Copy of" doc lines: `send_ex_command` is copied into at least nine, and `live_windows`, the poll and drain idioms and several `preview.rs` helpers into others. Nothing checks that a copy matches its original, or that the original still exists, so every deletion of a sibling's helper leaves the same trap R1 hit. `crates/wolf-ffi/tests/common/mod.rs` already exists and is in every real-Neovim target's `srcs`.
- **Spec §8 was stale before the task, and plan 1 kept it (review 1 R2).** The GNOME shared step in `specs/command-lifecycle.md` §8 never named the `CASES` row that `ui_action_table` requires, though the instructions listed it. Plan 1 §5 reused that bullet's text, the gap passed `coder-1` and `technical-writer-1`, and `coder-2` ($0.91) added the bullet. The cause was a stale spec this task fixed, so nothing further is recommended for it.
- **Plan 1's Swift test checked an adjacent observable (review 1 N1).** `testEveryRegistryDiscriminantDecodes` asserted only that each discriminant decodes to some case, so a stray arm to an existing case passed. Plan 2 made it map the case back through `wolf_ffi_command_kind`, and falsifier C5c showed only that test catches it. Nothing in the repository invited this; it was a test-design slip caught in review.
- **Part of the task could not be delivered.** The instructions asked for a rule in `CLAUDE.md`, which `.claude/skills/regina/SKILL.md` already says a task never asks for, since no agent writes it. Plan 1 D6 handed it to the user at no rework cost, but the rule is still missing, and review 2 approved with it outstanding.
- **Three defects landed and were left, as reviews 1 and 2 noted.** `specs/command-lifecycle.md` §4 says `from_command`'s match makes "adding or reordering a variant" a compile error, but a reorder fails `//crates/wolf-ffi:commands` instead; the task did not open §4. `specs/architecture.md`'s "the front-end never learns the ids" is loose, since `wolf_ffi_command_kind(id)` takes an id across the bridge for the tests. `crates/wolf-gnome/src/command_action.rs:585` is a 129-character string continuation that no checker flags.

## Recommendations

1. **Move the copied `wolf-ffi` test helpers into `crates/wolf-ffi/tests/common/mod.rs`.** Start with `send_ex_command`, the command-kind poll in `full_width.rs` and `first_drained_kind` in `commands.rs`, then `live_windows` and the `preview.rs` helpers. Delete each "Copy of" doc line as its helper moves. Review 1 R1 came from a copy whose original was deleted, and one shared helper cannot outlive itself.
2. **Add the registry rule to `CLAUDE.md` § Code comments, as the user's edit.** Put it after the paragraph ending "it goes false when the next one is added", in plan 1 §5's wording: "Nor does a comment or a doc restate a registry's length, its last entry or a range of its discriminants, as in "`0` … `30`" or "`NEW_WINDOW` .. `ZOOM_PANE`". It names or links the registry instead, since the next entry appended makes the restatement false." It extends the caller-count rule of [ghostty-5](ghostty-5.md) recommendation 6, and without it nothing stops the removed copies from returning.
3. **Open the two stale spec sentences in one task.** In `specs/command-lifecycle.md` §4, say `from_command`'s exhaustive match makes a new variant a compile error until it has a constant, and that a reorder fails `//crates/wolf-ffi:commands`. In `specs/architecture.md`, narrow "the front-end never learns the ids" to the dispatch path. `technical-writer-1`, `coder-1` and both reviews flagged the first, and every agent that reads §4 is told something the tree contradicts.
