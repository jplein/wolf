# Retrospective: the preview's code block takes Lighthouse's `slightHighlightBG`

Last verified at commit `7057d2ee2ab76f230d2d5b68eb99b120fe7642b9`.

The task changed the dark and light `pre` backgrounds in `crates/wolf-core/src/markdown/themes/default.css` to `#252525` and `#ffffff`, pinned both palettes in a CSS test and a new two-launch cage smoke, and recorded where the values come from in the markdown README. It took eight invocations at about $15.46: `architect-1` $5.70, `test-writer-1` $2.46, `coder-1` $1.02, `architect-2` $1.08, `test-writer-2` $0.76, `coder-2` $0.91, `technical-writer-1` $0.72 and `reviewer-1` $2.81. It had one round trip, `coder-1`'s dispute of the new smoke, and both gates passed on the first run.

## What went well

- **The theme's CSS was cheap to pin.** `markdown_render.rs` already had `theme_css` and `rule_decls`, and `style_block`'s doc named `@media` as the construct to read around. `test-writer-1` wrote the two-palette test by splitting at the media line, and `coder-1` took C1a, C1b and C1c in three macOS runs with no rework.
- **The appearance smoke's pieces could be reused as they stood.** The new smoke runs `PREVIEW_THEME_SCENARIO` unchanged, with `smoke_config_dir_at`, `APPEARANCE_FORCED_DARK` and `APPEARANCE_FORCED_LIGHT`. Plan 1 needed only three items of `preview.rs` made `pub(crate)`, and nothing in the shared P3 fixture moved.
- **The readings print paid for itself again.** `test-writer-1` added a `readings:` line in the convention [tabs-and-preview-4](tabs-and-preview-4.md) relied on, with its own addition of the unbridged "own strips of any height". That line showed `coder-1` the split at row 313 on the first green run. It also let `coder-2` read both of the bridge's margins, a 1-row ink gap and 16-row painted gaps, from the green run and G1c.
- **The highlighting-stylesheet check was one read.** `style_css` in `markdown.rs` emits only `color`, `font-weight`, `font-style` and `text-decoration`, and `HighlightStyle` has no background field. Plan 1, `coder-1` and `reviewer-1` each settled the question from that one function and `base.css`'s `pre code` rule.

## What did not go well

- **The one round trip came from a pixel premise no agent before the coder could measure.** Plan 1 required each fence line to be at least 32 characters so that G1c could fire, while assertion 6 counted rows by `row_is_majority`'s strict majority. With ink across most of the sampled columns, row 313 held 160 of 354 `pre` pixels, so `coder-1` read two runs where one was expected and returned `rejected`.
- **The fix was a bridge over short gaps.** `architect-2` chose the coder's option 1, `test-writer-2` added `CODEBG_MAX_INK_GAP_ROWS` and `bridged_majority_runs`, and `coder-2` re-took G1a. The round cost about $1.84 for `architect-2` and `test-writer-2`, plus `coder-1`'s two Linux runs of a smoke that could not pass.
- **`row_is_majority`'s doc invited the premise.** Its doc says that "all" would be defeated by the code glyphs and a strict majority is the remedy, with no word on lines that reach past the sampled midpoint. `architect-1`'s transcript shows it read `row_is_majority` and `majority_colour_runs` before writing the 32-character bound. The codehl smoke's fixture passes only because its lines leave the background a majority on every row, and nothing says so.
- **A throwaway PNG decoder was written a third time.** To get per-row hit counts, which the readings line does not print, `coder-1` wrote a pure-Python PNG reader into its scratch directory and decoded both kept captures. The same was done in [tabs-and-preview-4](tabs-and-preview-4.md) by its `coder-1` and in [regina-land-terminfo-2](regina-land-terminfo-2.md) by its `coder-2`.
- **The pinned `lighthouse.lua` was found by four different routes.** `architect-1` fetched it from GitHub twice. `coder-1` searched the Bazel cache, found nothing and took the plan's word for the values, while `technical-writer-1` and `reviewer-1` found it under the Bazel output tree after the macOS gate had built it. This is recommendation 1 of [regina-land-header](regina-land-header.md), come up again.
- **A Linux test flaked under the gate's load.** `coder-2`'s first `./gates.sh` on Linux failed in `wolf-core --test drawer_last_pane` alone, with a 10 s Neovim timeout in `bd_of_a_modified_buffer_beside_the_drawer_is_refused_with_e89` and a refused quit in `wq_beside_the_drawer_writes_and_closes_the_tab`. The binary loads no CSS, it passed alone in under 2 s, and the re-run passed, so it cost about 14 minutes of `coder-2`'s worker time and nothing in this change invited it.
- **Plan 1 asked for a count in a doc comment.** `markdown_render.rs` line 479 calls the new split "the second way around" `@media`, and line 668 says a rule "is read one of two ways around it". `reviewer-1` noted that a third technique has to reword both. It is the same kind of count that [tabs-and-preview-2](tabs-and-preview-2.md) recommendation 3 asks doc-lint to flag.

## Recommendations

1. **State the strict-majority limit where the helper is.** In `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/smoke_headless/preview_render.rs`, add to `row_is_majority`'s doc that a line whose ink reaches past the sampled columns' midpoint can leave the background a minority on its densest rows, and link `bridged_majority_runs` as the locator for such a fixture. `architect-1` read this doc before planning the 32-character bound, and the sentence would have put the bridge into plan 1, saving the $1.84 round.
2. **Print per-row hits when a run count is wrong.** In `preview_render.rs`, let `assert_code_bg_launch`'s assertion 6 message, and the codehl smoke's equivalent, list the hit count of each row in a gap between runs and its threshold. `coder-1` built a PNG decoder for exactly that table, as two earlier tasks did for theirs, and with it the dispute would have needed no scratch tool.
3. **Say how to read the pinned Lighthouse file.** This is recommendation 1 of [regina-land-header](regina-land-header.md), come up again: `architect-1`'s two fetches and `coder-1`'s unverified values both came from its absence. Every colour task plans against this file.
4. **Look at `drawer_last_pane`'s timeouts under load.** In `crates/wolf-core/tests/drawer_last_pane.rs`, check whether the 10 s probe-file wait in the `:bd` case and the `:wq` readback race the gate's parallel `cargo test`. One flake cost a full Linux gate run here, and no earlier report records it.
5. **Drop the count from `markdown_render.rs`'s `@media` docs.** Reword line 668 as "a theme rule is read around it instead:" and line 479 as "a way around the construct `style_block` names", as review 1 proposes. A third technique then adds a bullet without editing two other comments.
