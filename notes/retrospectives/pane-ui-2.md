# Task 2 of pane-ui: make the GNOME terminal drawer's divider a draggable `gtk::Paned`

Last verified at commit `a35962d865fc8f859bfa2f492a71c7154f7b4706`.

The task replaced `drawer_split` and `DrawerSplit` in `crates/wolf-gnome/src/drawer.rs` with a per-window `DrawerShare`, held by `TabsInner` in `tab_view.rs` and applied by a vertical `gtk::Paned` that `pane_view.rs` builds only while a drawer is bound. It added `tests/drawer_share.rs` (C1 to C5) and the cage smoke `smoke_headless/drawer_divider.rs` (C6a to C10). The commits are `2946f33` (code and tests) and `a35962d` (documentation).

Five invocations cost about $20.12 and 59 minutes: `architect-1` $6.46, `test-writer-1` $4.20, `coder-1` $4.00, `technical-writer-1` $2.39 and `reviewer-1` $3.07. There was no round trip. Both gates passed on both workers on their first run, and review 1 approved with minor notes.

## What went well

- **The pure divider arithmetic was already in `geometry.rs`.** `divider_position` and `divider_span_fraction` carry the span denominator and the GTK clamp for the tree's dividers, so `DrawerShare::position` and `drag_to` are one call each, and plan 1 §4 could give literal expectations for all eight fast-tier falsifiers. Each went red on its own edit in `coder-1`'s run. Keep new divider kinds on these two functions rather than their own arithmetic.
- **The tree dividers' guard was reusable.** `coder-1` lifted `DividerGuard::{new, apply, drag_span}` out of `install_paned_position`, and the drawer's handlers reuse its `applying` check and staleness gate. That is what makes the position immune to the `max-position` snap-back the instructions worried about, and C6a and C8 prove it.
- **The note that forbade the divider recorded its reasons.** The "No divider separates the two" paragraph of `notes/wolf-gnome/window-structure.md` said why a handle could not work: no place for its position, and a snap-back on the next `max-position` notify. The instructions could reverse the decision by answering each reason, and plan 1 kept the ones that still hold.
- **Two earlier recommendations paid off.** `smoke_headless` is a directory of feature modules, as [ghostty-4](ghostty-4.md) recommendation 2 asked, so the new smoke is one file and one `mod` line in `main.rs`, and the harness pieces it reused (`pin_terminal_smoke_shell`, `drawer_strip_edges`) needed only `pub(crate)`. Plan 1 §3 and §5 gave the word counts of the `drawer.rs` (207) and `pane_view.rs` (250) entries from `markdown_check.sh --entry-words`, and `technical-writer-1` had no overrun.
- **`regina on linux` carried every Linux-only step.** wolf-gnome has no Bazel target, and `coder-1` ran the pre-code red, the 14 falsifiers, the existing smokes and `./gates.sh` through it. Review 1 matched every red against the command logs without running anything.

## What did not go well

- **The plan's "store the paned" step hid a GTK teardown order, and the fix is recorded only in a comment.** `gtk_paned_dispose` unparents the children it still names, so a `drawer_divider` field holding the old paned past `rebuild_pane_area`'s re-parenting took the editor host back out. `coder-1`'s first smoke run went red at A3 with the plan's code, and it moved the reset to the top of `rebuild_pane_area`. Its report asked for this to be recorded, but `technical-writer-1` left it in the two-line comment because the `pane_view.rs` entry is at its budget.
- **The instructions took `DrawerState` to be per window.** It is per tab, one per `PaneInner`, and plan 1 §1 found that by reading `tab_view.rs` and moved the share to `TabsInner`. No document states it: `specs/terminal-panes.md` says the drawer session is "per window on GNOME", and the `drawer.rs` entry of `crates/wolf-gnome/src/README.md` says only that `pane_view.rs` holds `DrawerState` in a `Cell`. The landed entry still does not say it.
- **`pane_view.rs` was read in ranges by every agent.** It is 5,593 lines and 269 KB, and the drawer's glue is spread across `rebuild_pane_area`, `apply_drawer_frame`, `clear_drawer`, the render wake and the three new divider functions near line 4,330. `architect-1`, the costliest invocation, made 15 calls on the file, eight of them `sed` ranges, and `coder-1` made 19, six of them `Read` ranges. Its README entry is at 250 of 250 words, and `tab_view.rs`'s at 249.
- **The wolf-gnome READMEs are far over the size warning.** The documentation gate's Linux run warned that `crates/wolf-gnome/src/README.md` is 91,523 bytes and `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md` 111,778, from 91,220 and 110,337 at the base. [ghostty-4](ghostty-4.md) recorded the same growth.
- **Review 1 left three notes that landed as they are.** The drawer side of the floor has no end-to-end falsifier: `set_shrink_end_child(true)` at `pane_view.rs:4338` turns nothing red, which the tests README lists as unproven. The comment at `pane_view.rs:3631` keeps a count, "The one place the widget tree knows the drawer", which `CLAUDE.md` forbids. A shrink-then-grow toplevel resize is a check only a person can run.
- **`./gates.sh` ran four times.** `coder-1` ran it on both workers, then the implementation gate ran it on both again, while this task's only macOS change was one doc-comment line in `DrawerLayout.swift`. This is the pattern [open-items-1](open-items-1.md) and [round-3-6](round-3-6.md) recorded.

## Recommendations

1. **Record the `GtkPaned` dispose order in the notes.** In `notes/wolf-gnome/window-structure.md`, beside the paragraph on the drawer's paned, add that a `gtk::Paned` held outside the widget tree must be dropped before its children are re-parented, because its dispose unparents them. `coder-1`'s first smoke red came from plan 1 not knowing this, and the zoom tasks of this request (u03, u04) are likely to hold widgets aside across a rebuild in the same way.
2. **Say which drawer state is per tab and which is per window.** In the same note, state that `DrawerState` is one per tab's `PaneInner` while the drawer session and `DrawerShare` are one per window. Plan 1 had to correct the instructions on this by reading code, and the spec's "per window on GNOME" invites the same reading again.
3. **Split the drawer and divider glue out of `pane_view.rs` and its entry.** This is the wolf-gnome side of recommendation 1 of [pane-ui-1](pane-ui-1.md), which asked the same of `PaneContainerView.swift`. Move `DividerGuard`, `install_paned_position`, `build_drawer_paned`, `install_drawer_divider` and `reapply_drawer_divider` into a submodule with its own README entry. That gives the next divider change one file to read and frees words in the `pane_view.rs` entry for the gotcha that this task left in a comment, before u02 to u04 edit the same file.
