# Task 1 of pane-ui: make the macOS terminal drawer's top edge drag-resizable

Last verified at commit `70415195cd7aca166059f1fe1439934d24eb2f19`.

The task gave `PaneContainerView` a per-view `drawerWeight` that a drag on the drawer's top edge sets, and gave `DrawerLayout` one `minPaneHeight` floor for both sides plus a pure `draggedWeight(pointerY:paneAreaHeight:minPaneHeight:)`. It added `TerminalDrawerResizeTests.swift` (T1 to T3) and K1 to K8 in `DrawerLayoutTests.swift`, and one paragraph in `specs/terminal-panes.md`. The commits are `72acdd7` (code, tests and spec) and `7041519` (documentation).

Five invocations cost about $11.95 and 38 minutes: `architect-1` $3.65, `test-writer-1` $1.78, `coder-1` $2.26, `technical-writer-1` $1.88 and `reviewer-1` $2.38. There was no round trip. Both gates passed on both workers on their first run, and review 1 approved with minor notes.

## What went well

- **One sizing path carried the session and grid resizes.** `syncGridSizes()` already sizes every pane, so the drawer session's `resize_terminal` and every pane's `ResizeGrid` followed a drag with no new plumbing. The change to `PaneContainerView.swift` was 68 lines, and C1d and C1e showed T1 going red when either half of that path was cut. Keep new pane kinds sized through `syncGridSizes()` rather than a path of their own.
- **The pure `DrawerLayout` seam made the arithmetic cheap to plan and test.** The weight was already a parameter at the one call site, so plan 1 could check its new formula against each existing `DrawerLayoutTests` expectation by hand, and K1 to K8 run headlessly. Every K falsifier, C4 to C11, went red on its own edit.
- **The live fixtures reached the mouse handlers directly.** `TerminalViewFixture`, `fixture.hostContainer(tabpage:)` and `@testable import WolfMacOS` let T1 to T3 call `mouseDown`, `mouseDragged` and `mouseUp` on a real core, Neovim and shell. The new file compiled against the base code, and `coder-1` ran T1 to T3 red on behaviour (the drawer top stuck at 487) before writing any code.
- **The architect counted the entry budgets before assigning words.** Plan 1 ran `markdown_check.sh --entry-words` and gave the `DrawerLayout.swift` entry at 246 of 250 and the `PaneContainerView.swift` entry at 250 of 250, with a net-neutral instruction for each. `technical-writer-1` had no overrun. This is the use [regina-land-header](regina-land-header.md) recorded.

## What did not go well

- **`PaneContainerView.swift` was read by every agent again.** It is 3,195 lines and 152,263 bytes. `architect-1`, the costliest invocation, read it with one `cat -n` and seven more `sed` ranges, `test-writer-1` printed the whole file and then reread three ranges, and `coder-1` read five ranges of it. The change touched only drawer state, hit-testing and the drag handlers.
- **The documents this change touched were at their limits again.** To add one clause, `technical-writer-1` cut consumer names from three unrelated bullets of the `PaneContainerView.swift` entry (review 1 §5). `crates/wolf-macos/Sources/WolfKeyMap/README.md` crossed the 50,000-byte `big-file` warning during the edit and was trimmed back to 49,992 bytes, 8 under it, and `crates/wolf-macos/Sources/WolfMacOS/README.md` stands at 49,814.
- **The new spec sentence overstates the layout.** `specs/terminal-panes.md` says that neither the drawer nor the editor tree goes below one pane's minimum height. `DrawerLayout.init` lets the drawer take the whole area when the area cannot hold two floors, so a dragged share of 0.895 at an area of 120 and a floor of 63 gives the editor 13. Review 1 §2 and `technical-writer-1` both saw this and left it, since `draggedWeight` returns `nil` there and the sentence can be read as being about dragging only.
- **A falsifier anchor was not unique.** Plan 1 described C1e in words, and `coder-1`'s script anchored it on `guard let grid = panes[id]?.boundGrid else { continue }`, which occurs twice in `PaneContainerView.swift`, at lines 1253 and 2937. The script stopped at C1e, and the rerun repeated C1a to C1d, eight more Bazel test runs. This is the pattern [fixes-1](fixes-1.md) and [fixes-2](fixes-2.md) recorded.
- **The spec is ahead of wolf-gnome until u01 lands.** The drawer paragraph is platform-neutral, as the task instructions ask, but the GNOME drawer has a fixed share, as `notes/wolf-gnome/window-structure.md` and the `drawer.rs` entry of `crates/wolf-gnome/src/README.md` say (review 1 §6). This is planned, but a cleanup round between the two tasks would see a spec and a front-end that disagree.

## Recommendations

1. **Split the drawer out of `PaneContainerView.swift` and its entry.** This is recommendation 1 of [open-items-9](open-items-9.md) and recommendation 1 of [nerd-font-2](nerd-font-2.md), come up again. Here the change was drawer state, an edge hit-test and the drag branch, all of which would sit in a `PaneContainerView+Drawer.swift` extension with its own README entry, and every agent read the whole 3,195-line file to find them.
2. **Make room in the `WolfKeyMap` README before its next entry.** This is recommendation 5 of [titlebar-colours-2](titlebar-colours-2.md), come up again. The file is 8 bytes under the warning after this task, so the next `WolfKeyMap` change, such as the split or zoom tasks of this request, will start by trimming unrelated entries.
3. **Hold the drawer at its floor in an area too short for two floors.** In `DrawerLayout.init` in `crates/wolf-macos/Sources/WolfKeyMap/DrawerLayout.swift`, ignore `weight` when `paneAreaHeight < 2 * floor`, as `draggedWeight` already does, and add a K case at an area of 120. That makes the spec sentence true of the layout as well as the drag, which review 1 §2 and `technical-writer-1` each had to reason through and leave.
