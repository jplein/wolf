# Retrospective: Preview and Edit in Another Pane reuse the other pane on Linux

Last verified at commit `f739abfe0f0088de692c6da4ff3cf86ddc8256c9`.

The task made wolf-gnome's Preview in Another Pane and Edit in Another Pane target an existing pane, by porting macOS's `OtherPaneSeam` to `crates/wolf-gnome/src/other_pane_seam.rs`. It also added a macOS flow test, `OtherPaneFlowTests.swift`, which shows that macOS already reuses the pane in both of the user's scenarios.

The eight invocations cost about $23.52 and 58 minutes of agent time. `architect-1` cost $8.04 over 99 turns, `test-writer-1` $5.04, `coder-1` $3.09, `reviewer-1` $2.98 and `technical-writer-1` $1.87. There was one round trip, and the rework it added cost $1.70.

## What went well

- **The macOS seam and its table test were a ready specification.** `OtherPaneSeam.swift` and `OtherPaneSeamTests.swift` gave the rule and eight cases, and wolf-gnome's `PaneTree` in `layout.rs` already had `neighbor` and `leaves`. `coder-1` wrote the 74-line port in one `Write`, and all eight T1 cases and every falsifier from C1a to C5 went red under its edit at the first try.
- **The port convention kept the wording check quiet without an allowlist entry.** `notes/wolf-gnome-decisions.md` and `tools/lint/wolf_gnome_wording_check.sh` told `coder-1` how a port of a `WolfKeyMap` seam is written. It ran the check once, and the `wolf-gnome-wording` step of `implementation-1` on Linux exited 0.
- **The word-count mode of the markdown checker was used before writing.** `architect-1` ran `./tools/lint/markdown_check.sh --entry-words` on the READMEs it planned to grow, so plan 1 could warn that the `pane_view.rs` entry stood at 248 of 250 words. `technical-writer-1` made its cuts with no failed gate. That mode is recommendation 4 of [ghostty-4](ghostty-4.md), carried out.
- **An existing FFI test pinned the focus half.** `crates/wolf-ffi/tests/other_pane.rs` already covers which pane core's Lua focuses after each command. So plan 1 gave T2 and T3's focus assertions no falsifier of their own, and `review-1` accepted that on the strength of that file.
- **Both gates passed at the first run on both workers.** `implementation-1` and `documentation-1` each exited 0 on macOS and Linux.

## What did not go well

- **A test premise copied from an existing fixture cost the one round trip.** `test-writer-1` waited for `:edit a.md` in T6/T7 with `handle.is_markdown_active()`, as `PreviewViewFixture.swift` line 65 does after its own `:edit`. A fresh core's startup buffer is the Markdown-named `Untitled.md`, so the wait held at once and the first press previewed `Untitled.md`. `coder-1` ran T6/T7 first, saw both fail on the premise, and returned `rejected`; `architect-2` ($1.31) wrote plan 2 and `test-writer-2` ($0.39) fixed the waits.
- **The fact that would have prevented it was written down many times, in none of the places the writer read.** `crates/wolf-ffi/tests/README.md` states three times that `is_markdown_active()` proves nothing after a Markdown `:e`, and `crates/wolf-core/tests/README.md` names the startup `Untitled.md` in six entries. Neither the `is_markdown_active` entry of `crates/wolf-ffi/src/README.md` nor, before this task, `crates/wolf-macos/Tests/WolfMacOSTests/README.md` said it. `technical-writer-1` added a seventh copy to the latter, and `PreviewViewFixture.swift` keeps the weak wait at `HEAD`.
- **The startup buffer has now tripped three tasks.** [open-items-14](open-items-14.md) and [round-3-8](round-3-8.md) each traced a flake or a falsifier to a first action meeting the startup `Untitled.md`. Here it was not a race but a wrong premise, and the cause is the same: a test that reads "a Markdown buffer is active" as "my file is open".
- **The architect still has no `regina on linux`.** `architect-1` grepped `build.md`, the regina skill and an earlier report for `ssh` and the Linux worker in four calls, before it planned which Linux commands the coder runs. This is recommendation 1 of [round-3-2](round-3-2.md), come up again, and here it cost little.
- **The plan cost a third of the task, much of it reading one smoke file.** `architect-1` read about 1,500 lines of the 4,643-line `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/smoke_headless/preview.rs` with `sed`, to plan the rewrite of T4 and T5. Those smokes number their assertions and rows in doc tables with "measured" cells, so `test-writer-1` renumbered T5's rows 7 to 9 and rewrote their messages by hand. `review-1` found T5's row 8 "measured" cell still carries the reading from the three-pane layout.
- **The wolf-gnome READMEs grew further past the size warning.** `crates/wolf-gnome/src/README.md` went from 86,220 to 87,858 bytes and `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/README.md` from 101,148 to 102,054, both over the `big-file` warning in `documentation-1`. [ghostty-4](ghostty-4.md) recorded the same two files at 85,910 and 94,173.
- **Three notes were edited and none was re-verified.** `test-writer-1` rewrote a paragraph of `notes/crates/wolf-gnome/tests/smoke_headless/preview.rs.md`, and `technical-writer-1` one each of `notes/wolf-gnome/present-limitations.md` and `notes/crates/wolf-gnome/src/app.rs.md`. Each left the SHA, as `CLAUDE.md` asks of a check that is not whole-file. Recommendation 5 of [ghostty-4](ghostty-4.md) named this cost for the decisions note.
- **The landed work leaves the side of each shell press unpinned.** `review-1` found that swapping `Side::Right` and `Side::Left` in `AppShell::preview_in_other_pane` and `edit_in_other_pane` keeps every test green, because every live scenario has two side-by-side panes. It also found `NO_OTHER_PANE_REASON` in `shell.rs` narrower than the `None` it reports, and a count ("the one that discriminates the pin") in the `DPI_PIN_STDERR_PREFIX` doc at `preview.rs` line 1130.

## Recommendations

1. **Say once, where a consumer looks, that `is_markdown_active()` does not mean a file opened.** Put the gotcha on the `is_markdown_active` entry of `crates/wolf-ffi/src/README.md`, and replace the copies in `crates/wolf-ffi/tests/README.md`, `crates/wolf-core/tests/README.md` and `crates/wolf-macos/Tests/WolfMacOSTests/README.md` with a link. Evidence: the T6/T7 rejection, $1.70 and a second plan.
2. **Fix the wait in `PreviewViewFixture.swift`.** After its `:edit` of `probe.md`, wait for the active window to show `probe.md`, as `OtherPaneFlowTests.swift` now does. `test-writer-1` copied the weak wait from this fixture, and the next test writer who reads it will copy it again.
3. **Give the architect `regina on linux`.** This is recommendation 1 of [round-3-2](round-3-2.md), come up again in `architect-1`'s search.
4. **Name the assertions of the long preview smokes instead of numbering them.** In `crates/wolf-gnome/tests/smoke_headless/preview.rs`, key T4's and T5's assertions and doc-table rows by a short name, so a step inserted in the middle renumbers nothing. Drop the "measured" cells, or move them to the mirror note with the commit they were measured at. `test-writer-1`'s renumbering and `review-1`'s stale row 8 are the evidence.
5. **Split the wolf-gnome READMEs.** Give the preview files a directory and README of their own, as `src/terminal/README.md` did for the terminal files, which [ghostty-4](ghostty-4.md) recorded as the shape to follow. Both READMEs grew again here, and every agent of this task read them.
6. **Pin the side each shell press passes.** Add a three-in-a-row case to the wolf-gnome preview smokes, pressing from the middle pane, so a swapped `Side` turns red. In the same edit, widen `NO_OTHER_PANE_REASON` to the unbound-window case and drop "the one" from the `DPI_PIN_STDERR_PREFIX` doc. These are `review-1`'s notes.
