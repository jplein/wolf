# Task 2 of nerd-font: cascade Symbols Nerd Font Mono behind every macOS grid face

Last verified at commit `16805c10a456dc0c14b6761dc551a9b4ac3fd2f2`.

The task gave each of the four faces `CellMetrics` builds a CoreText cascade list of the bundled symbols face, then the system list, with metrics read from the plain face. It also made `PaneContainerView.paintCells` fill a row's backgrounds before its glyphs, and added `SymbolsFallbackTests.swift` with nine CoreText and live-Neovim tests. It ran straight through in five invocations and about $13.77: `architect-1` $3.67, `test-writer-1` $2.62, `coder-1` $2.36, `technical-writer-1` $2.24 and `reviewer-1` $2.89.

There was no round trip. No review rejected, no gate failed, and no discrepancy or resume occurred. Both gates passed on both workers on their first run, and review 1 approved with minor notes.

## What went well

- **Existing test fixtures carried the new tests.** `LiveCore.resolveRunfile` and the `WOLF_TEST_LIGHTHOUSE_DIR` wiring in `crates/wolf-macos/BUILD.bazel` gave the three font trees a pattern to copy, and `TerminalViewFixture` gave T9 a live Neovim grid with no new helper. `test-writer-1` added the staging in `LiveCore.swift` and three `data` and `env` lines.
- **Plan 1 named every new API the tests call, so tests and code met at the first try.** `test-writer-1` compiled its file against a temporary stub of `FontRegistration.symbolsFamily`, `registerFonts(in:)` and `measured(family:…)`, wired into `srcs` for one 81-second build and then removed. `coder-1`'s baseline had 24 failures, all in `SymbolsFallbackTests`. This is the pattern of [open-items-5](open-items-5.md) and [round-3-7](round-3-7.md) again.
- **The terminal painter already used the needed paint order.** `TerminalPaneView.paintRow` fills a row's backgrounds before its glyphs, so plan 1 could point `paintCells` at it as the model, and C9a showed the grid test going red without the change.
- **The pin README's measurements caught a wrong plan sentence.** Plan 1 §5 asked the writer to say that an unscaled Mono glyph stays within one em. The "Why the Mono face" section of `bazel/nerd-fonts-symbols/README.md`, written by task 1, says 850 outlines reach past their advance, and `technical-writer-1` declined the sentence on that evidence.

## What did not go well

- **The `PaneContainerView.swift` entry was at its word budget again.** Plan 1 §5 gave its entry in `crates/wolf-macos/Sources/WolfMacOS/README.md` a paint-order bullet. `architect-1` read the entry but never ran `markdown_check.sh --entry-words`, and the entry stood at 250 of 250. `technical-writer-1` dropped the bullet and put the rule in the `GlyphPainter.swift` entry, which review 1 accepted.
- **The whole README is nearly full.** After this task, ten entries of `crates/wolf-macos/Sources/WolfMacOS/README.md` are at 243 to 250 of 250 words, including `CellMetrics.swift` at 248. The file is 49,805 bytes against the checker's 50,000-byte warning. `technical-writer-1` cut the existing `CellMetrics.swift` and `LiveCore.swift` text to fit, and left out the "no static cache" rule and the reason for D6 for lack of words.
- **A comma list in `--test_filter` ran no test.** `coder-1`'s first C2a and C4a runs passed a comma-separated filter to `//crates/wolf-macos:WolfMacOSTests`, which executes zero tests and reports "No tests were executed" as a failure. Both runs were discarded and redone one method each. `build.md` still does not name `--test_filter`, and `coder-1` listed this under what made the task harder.
- **T6 fails through a process trap, not its assertion.** `compare` in `SymbolsFallbackTests.swift` uses `precondition` on the two drawings' sizes. A moved `cellWidth` resizes the bitmaps, so C6a's red was a "Trace/breakpoint trap" that ends the XCTest process before T6's `XCTAssertEqual`s and hides every later test's result. `coder-1` and review 1 both flagged it, and it landed unchanged.
- **A doc comment in another module describes `paintCells`'s loop shape.** The `paneGlyphLoop` case in `crates/wolf-macos/Sources/WolfKeyMap/FrameStats.swift` still says "the `for row` / `for col` double loop", which the two-pass rewrite made false. Plan 1 did not list it, and only review 1 found it.
- **Other drift was found and left for later.** Review 1 named the Tests README intro, which says `SymbolsFallbackTests.swift` draws with no view though T9 builds a live grid. It also named the history word the history phrase "now consumed" in the `font` doc comment of `CellMetrics.swift`, and "both font families" in `specs/architecture.md`, stale since task 1 bundled a third.
- **The overlays' missing cascade is recorded nowhere in the repository.** `overlayFont(metrics:)` in `CmdlineView.swift` builds an `NSFont` from the family name, so the command line, popup and toasts get no symbols cascade. `technical-writer-1` and review 1 both noted it as a follow-up, but the Present limitations in `notes/wolf-macos-decisions.md` do not mention it.
